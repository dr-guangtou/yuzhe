## Short Summary

This landmark HSC-SSP study reveals, for the first time with unprecedented statistical power and imaging fidelity, that the galaxy size–mass relation is not a single power law but breaks at a physically meaningful pivot mass—where the transition from in situ star formation to dry merger dominance occurs—and crucially shows that low-mass quiescent galaxies retain the large sizes of their star-forming progenitors, providing direct structural evidence for recent, gentle quenching without major structural disruption.

**中文：** 这项具有里程碑意义的HSC-SSP研究首次以空前的统计效力与成像保真度揭示：星系尺寸–质量关系并非单一幂律，而是在一个具有明确物理意义的拐点质量处发生转折——该拐点对应于原位恒星形成主导向干并合主导转变的临界质量；尤为关键的是，该研究证实低质量宁静星系仍保留其恒星形成前身星系所具有的较大尺寸，从而为近期发生的、温和的熄灭过程（未引发显著结构扰动）提供了直接的结构证据。

## Detailed Summary

This study addresses a long-standing limitation in galaxy evolution studies: the scarcity of statistically robust, high-fidelity size measurements across broad stellar mass and redshift ranges—particularly for low-mass quiescent galaxies, whose structural properties have remained poorly constrained due to observational challenges. Prior work often relied on small, heterogeneous samples or shallow imaging that blurred distinctions between star-forming and quiescent populations, obscuring how structural scaling relations evolve with mass and environment. By leveraging the unprecedented combination of depth, resolution, and survey area of the Hyper Suprime-Cam Subaru Strategic Program (HSC-SSP) DR2, this work fills a critical gap—delivering the first mass-complete, million-galaxy characterization of the size–mass relation (R<sub>e</sub>–M<sub>*</sub>) over 0.2 < z < 1.0, with robust separation into star-forming and quiescent subpopulations. The scale and precision of this dataset enable, for the first time, a definitive test of whether the R<sub>e</sub>–M<sub>*</sub> relation is universally linear—or instead exhibits intrinsic curvature tied to physical transitions in galaxy growth modes.

The analysis employs a carefully constructed sample of ~1.5 million galaxies over ~100 deg<sup>2</sup>, complete to log(M<sub>*</sub>/M<sub>⊙</sub>) > 10.2 (9.2) out to z = 1.0 (0.6), using high-S/N HSC g-, r-, i-, z-, and y-band imaging. Galaxy sizes (effective radii R<sub>e</sub>) are measured via multi-wavelength, point-spread-function-corrected Sérsic profile fits—capitalizing on HSC’s exceptional image quality (median seeing ~0.6″) to achieve sub-10% size uncertainties for galaxies down to R<sub>e</sub> ≈ 0.5 kpc at z ∼ 0.8. Stellar masses and star formation classifications are derived from SED fitting with consistent templates and dust attenuation laws, while rigorous completeness corrections and Monte Carlo simulations ensure robust quantification of selection effects and intrinsic scatter. Crucially, the team moves beyond simple linear fits by modeling the R<sub>e</sub>–M<sub>*</sub> relation as a broken power law—fitting both the pivot mass M<sub>p</sub> and the slopes above and below it independently for each population and redshift slice.

The results reveal a striking, mass-dependent architecture in galaxy structural scaling: both star-forming and quiescent galaxies follow broken power-law R<sub>e</sub>–M<sub>*</sub> relations, with a clear pivot at log(M<sub>p</sub>/M<sub>⊙</sub>) ≈ 10.2–10.6 for quiescent systems and ∼10.7 for star-forming ones—evolving modestly with redshift. Below M<sub>p</sub>, quiescent galaxies exhibit an extraordinarily shallow slope of R<sub>e</sub> ∝ M<sub>*</sub><sup>0.1</sup>, meaning their sizes barely increase with mass; remarkably, these low-mass quiescent galaxies are just as large as their star-forming counterparts of equal mass. This provides compelling empirical evidence that many low-mass quiescent galaxies were recently quenched—likely via environmentally driven mechanisms like ram-pressure stripping or harassment—without undergoing significant compaction or structural reorganization. Above M<sub>p</sub>, the slope steepens dramatically to R<sub>e</sub> ∝ M<sub>*</sub><sup>0.6–0.7</sup> for quiescent galaxies, signaling a shift toward dry mergers as the dominant growth channel. Most significantly, the pivot mass aligns precisely with the stellar mass where the quiescent fraction reaches 50%—a powerful convergence suggesting M<sub>p</sub> marks the fundamental transition in galaxy assembly: from in situ star formation–dominated growth at lower masses to merger-driven structural evolution at higher masses. This unified interpretation bridges morphology, kinematics, and star formation history into a coherent physical framework—and establishes the broken R<sub>e</sub>–M<sub>*</sub> relation as a new diagnostic tool for probing the interplay between quenching mechanisms, environmental influence, and hierarchical assembly across cosmic time.

### 中文版

本研究旨在解决星系演化研究中一个长期存在的瓶颈问题：在宽广的恒星质量与红移范围内，缺乏统计上可靠、高保真度的星系尺寸测量——尤其是对低质量宁静星系而言，其结构特性因观测难度而始终难以精确约束。以往工作往往依赖于小样本、异质性较强的样本，或深度不足的成像数据，导致恒星形成星系与宁静星系之间的结构差异被模糊化，从而掩盖了结构标度关系随质量与环境演化的本质规律。本研究充分利用Hyper Suprime-Cam Subaru Strategic Program（HSC-SSP）第二版数据发布（DR2）所具备的前所未有的深度、分辨率与巡天面积优势，填补了这一关键空白——首次构建出红移范围0.2 < z < 1.0内质量完备、涵盖百万量级星系的尺寸–质量关系（R<sub>e</sub>–M<sub>*</sub>）表征，并对恒星形成与宁静星系子样本实现了稳健分离。该数据集的规模与精度首次使我们得以明确检验：R<sub>e</sub>–M<sub>*</sub>关系是否具有普适线性形式；抑或其内在曲率真实反映了星系增长模式的物理转变。

本分析基于约150万个星系构成的精心构建样本，覆盖天区面积约100 deg<sup>2</sup>，在z = 1.0（z = 0.6）处分别达到log(M<sub>*</sub>/M<sub>⊙</sub>) > 10.2（9.2）的质量完备极限，所用数据为HSC g、r、i、z及y波段高信噪比成像。星系尺寸（有效半径R<sub>e</sub>）通过多波段、点扩散函数校正的Sérsic轮廓拟合获得——依托HSC卓越的成像质量（中值视宁度约0.6″），对红移z ∼ 0.8、R<sub>e</sub> ≈ 0.5 kpc的星系亦可实现优于10%的尺寸测量精度。恒星质量和恒星形成分类则通过光谱能量分布（SED）拟合统一获得，采用一致的模板与尘埃衰减律；同时，通过严格的完备性修正与蒙特卡洛模拟，确保对选择效应与本征弥散的量化具有高度鲁棒性。尤为关键的是，研究团队超越了简单的线性拟合范式，将R<sub>e</sub>–M<sub>*</sub>关系建模为分段幂律形式——对每一子样本及红移切片，独立拟合拐点质量M<sub>p</sub>以及拐点上下两侧的幂律斜率。

结果揭示出星系结构标度关系中一种显著的、依赖于质量的层级架构：恒星形成与宁静星系均遵循分段幂律型R<sub>e</sub>–M<sub>*</sub>关系，其中宁静星系的拐点质量位于log(M<sub>p</sub>/M<sub>⊙</sub>) ≈ 10.2–10.6，恒星形成星系则约为10.7，且该拐点仅随红移发生微弱演化。在M<sub>p</sub>以下，宁静星系呈现异常平缓的斜率R<sub>e</sub> ∝ M<sub>*</sub><sup>0.1</sup>，即其尺寸几乎不随质量增加；尤为引人注目的是，这些低质量宁静星系的尺寸与其同质量恒星形成星系几乎完全相当。这为“大量低质量宁静星系近期才经历熄灭”提供了强有力的实证支持——其熄灭机制很可能源于环境驱动过程，例如冲压剥离或星系际骚扰，且未伴随显著的致密化或结构重组。而在M<sub>p</sub>以上，宁静星系的斜率急剧增大至R<sub>e</sub> ∝ M<sub>*</sub><sup>0.6–0.7</sup>，标志着其主导增长通道已转向干并合。最具意义的是，拐点质量M<sub>p</sub>与宁静星系占比达50%所对应的恒星质量高度吻合——这一强有力的一致性表明，M<sub>p</sub>标志着星系组装模式的根本性转变：在较低质量端以原位恒星形成主导，在较高质量端则转为并合驱动的结构演化。这一统一物理解释成功将形态学、运动学与恒星形成历史整合进一个自洽的物理框架，并确立分段型R<sub>e</sub>–M<sub>*</sub>关系作为一种全新诊断工具，用于系统探查宇宙时标下熄灭机制、环境影响与等级式并合组装之间的内在关联。
