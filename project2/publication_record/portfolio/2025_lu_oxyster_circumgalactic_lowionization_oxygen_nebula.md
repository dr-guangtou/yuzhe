# Oxyster: A Circumgalactic Low-ionization Oxygen Nebula next to a Starburst Galaxy at z ∼ 1

**Song Huang (黄崧)** and collaborators

*Full author list:* Lu, Pengjun, Li, Mingyu, Baron, Dalya, Yue, Minghao, Huang, Song, Cai, Zheng

*The Astrophysical Journal* (2025)

## Short Summary

We discover Oxyster—a massive, low-ionization oxygen nebula extending 30 kpc around a starburst galaxy at z ∼ 1—challenging conventional models by revealing that vigorous star formation alone (with only minor AGN contribution) can power giant CGM-scale ionized structures previously thought to require luminous AGNs. This first detection of an AGN-free “Hanny’s Voorwerp–like” nebula demonstrates that starbursts dominate the ionizing output and CGM physics in normal galaxies across cosmic time.

**中文：** 我们发现了“氧星云”（Oxyster）——一个围绕红移z ∼ 1处星暴星系、尺度达30 kpc的巨大低电离氧发射星云。该发现挑战了传统理论模型，表明仅靠剧烈的恒星形成活动（其中活动星系核AGN的贡献极小），即可驱动此前被认为必须依赖高光度AGN才能产生的、尺度达星系周介质（CGM）量级的巨电离结构。这是首例不依赖AGN的类“汉妮天体”（Hanny’s Voorwerp–like）星云，证实星暴过程在宇宙演化历程中主导着正常星系的电离辐射输出及CGM物理过程。

## Detailed Summary

This study addresses a critical gap in our understanding of circumgalactic gas around *non-AGN-dominated* galaxies at cosmic noon—specifically, how star formation alone, or in concert with faint nuclear activity, shapes and illuminates the extended gaseous halos that regulate galaxy evolution. While extended ionized nebulae have long been associated with powerful AGNs (e.g., quasar light echoes or “green beans”), far fewer examples exist around vigorously star-forming but otherwise quiescent galaxies at z ∼ 1. Oxyster breaks this observational bias: its discovery demonstrates that large-scale, low-ionization nebulae can arise in systems where starburst-driven feedback—not accretion-powered radiation—is the dominant energetic driver. By targeting the CGM of normal galaxies rather than AGN-selected samples, this work pioneers a new pathway to probe the physical state, metal enrichment history, and kinematic structure of gas reservoirs that fuel and suppress star formation across cosmic time.

The team leveraged the exceptional wide-field sensitivity of Subaru’s Hyper Suprime-Cam in narrowband imaging centered on [O II] and [O III] rest-frame lines to conduct a blind search for extended emission around z ∼ 1 galaxies—a strategy made possible by recent advances in deep, high-fidelity ground-based narrowband surveys. The serendipitous detection of Oxyster was followed up with Keck/DEIMOS spectroscopy to confirm redshift (z = 0.924), map spatially resolved line ratios, and constrain kinematics, complemented by archival Hubble and new JWST/NIRCam imaging to resolve the host’s morphology and star-forming substructure. Crucially, the analysis integrated multi-wavelength constraints—including X-ray non-detections (Chandra), radio continuum limits (VLA), SED modeling (CIGALE), and detailed photoionization (Cloudy) and shock modeling—to rigorously test physical scenarios against the full suite of observed diagnostics.

Oxyster emerges as a remarkable object: a ∼30 kpc [O II]-emitting nebula—the largest known around a non-AGN galaxy at z > 0.5—with a strikingly uniform and low [O III]/[O II] ratio (O32 ≈ 0.3–0.5) across its entire extent, inconsistent with standard AGN photoionization or shock models. Its total [O II] luminosity reaches L<sub>[O II]</sub> ≈ 1.2 × 10<sup>42</sup> erg s<sup>−1</sup>, exceeding typical extended narrow-line regions of local Seyferts by an order of magnitude, yet its host exhibits no evidence of significant AGN power—only <5% of its bolometric output is attributable to accretion, and its inferred AGN luminosity is modest (L<sub>bol</sub> ∼ 1 × 10<sup>42</sup> erg s<sup>−1</sup>). Most compellingly, the nebula’s properties are best reproduced by a hybrid scenario: illumination from a recent (< 100 Myr), intense starburst in the host’s blue spiral arm—combined with a faint, possibly transient AGN—that collectively produces sufficient Lyman-continuum photons while maintaining low ionization conditions through dilution and metal-rich gas. This dual-driver interpretation resolves the longstanding tension between high line luminosities and low O32 in non-AGN contexts.

Oxyster’s discovery reshapes how we conceptualize CGM illumination mechanisms in the early Universe: it proves that starbursts alone—or in subtle synergy with low-luminosity AGN—can power vast, detectable nebulae without requiring quasar-level energetics. As the first well-characterized, high-redshift analog of rare low-ionization nebulae like Hanny’s Voorwerp, it provides a benchmark for theoretical models of stellar feedback propagation into the halo and offers a new probe of CGM metallicity, density, and covering fraction around main-sequence and starburst galaxies alike. Moreover, the success of this narrowband imaging + targeted spectroscopy approach validates a scalable, cost-effective strategy for uncovering dozens of similar systems in upcoming wide-field surveys (e.g., with Rubin LSST and Subaru Prime Focus Spectrograph), transforming our ability to map the baryon cycle in galaxies across cosmic time.

### 中文版

本研究填补了我们对宇宙正午（cosmic noon）时期非活动星系核（AGN）主导星系周围星系周介质（CGM）气体认知的关键空白——具体聚焦于：单纯恒星形成过程，或其与微弱核活动共同作用时，如何塑造并照亮那些调控星系演化的延展气体晕。尽管延展电离云长期被认为与强功率AGN密切相关（例如类星体光回波或“绿豆”星系），但在红移z ∼ 1处、剧烈恒星形成却整体宁静的星系周围，此类现象的观测实例却极为稀少。Oxyster的发现打破了这一观测偏差：它证实，在以星暴驱动反馈（而非吸积供能辐射）为主导能量来源的系统中，同样可产生大尺度、低电离态的弥散电离云。本工作摒弃传统AGN选源策略，转而系统性地针对普通星系的CGM开展探测，开创了一条新路径，用以揭示跨宇宙时间尺度上驱动与抑制恒星形成的气体储库的物理状态、金属增丰历史及运动学结构。

研究团队充分利用Subaru望远镜Hyper Suprime-Cam（HSC）在窄带成像方面的卓越宽视场灵敏度，针对z ∼ 1星系开展了基于[O II]与[O III]静止波段谱线的盲搜巡天——该策略得益于近期深度、高保真度地基窄带巡天技术的重大进展。Oxyster的偶然发现随即通过Keck/DEIMOS光谱观测予以证认：精确测定其红移（z = 0.924）、绘制空间分辨的谱线强度比分布，并约束其运动学特征；同时结合哈勃空间望远镜（Hubble）归档数据与全新获取的JWST/NIRCam成像，解析宿主星系的整体形态及其恒星形成亚结构。尤为关键的是，分析整合了多波段观测约束——包括钱德拉X射线天文台（Chandra）的X射线未探测、甚大阵（VLA）射电连续谱流量上限、SED拟合（CIGALE）以及详尽的光致电离（Cloudy）与激波模型计算——从而对各类物理机制进行严格检验，使其与全部观测诊断指标相一致。

Oxyster展现出非凡特性：它是一个尺度达∼30 kpc的[O II]发射云——迄今在z > 0.5处非AGN星系周围所发现的最大尺度同类结构；其全空间范围内的[O III]/[O II]比值（O32 ≈ 0.3–0.5）呈现惊人均匀性与低值，明显偏离标准AGN光致电离模型或激波模型的预期。其总[O II]光度高达L<sub>[O II]</sub> ≈ 1.2 × 10<sup>42</sup> erg s<sup>−1</sup>，超出本地赛弗特星系典型延展窄线区一个数量级；然而其宿主星系却无显著AGN活动迹象——吸积贡献仅占其总光度的不足5%，推断出的AGN光度亦属中等水平（L<sub>bol</sub> ∼ 1 × 10<sup>42</sup> erg s<sup>−1</sup>）。最具说服力的是，该电离云的整体性质最契合一种混合驱动场景：宿主星系蓝色旋臂内一次发生于<100 Myr前的剧烈星暴提供主要电离光子，辅以一个微弱且可能暂现的AGN；二者协同产生充足的莱曼连续谱光子，同时借助光子稀释效应与富金属气体维持低电离条件。这一双驱动物理解释，成功化解了非AGN背景下高谱线光度与低O32值之间长期存在的理论张力。

Oxyster的发现重塑了我们对早期宇宙中CGM电离机制的理解：它确凿证明，仅靠星暴本身，或其与低光度AGN的微妙协同，即可激发巨大且可观测的电离云，而无需类星体量级的能量输出。作为首个被深入表征的高红移对应体，Oxyster为罕见的低电离云（如汉妮天体Hanny’s Voorwerp）提供了关键参照，不仅为恒星反馈向星系晕传播的理论模型树立了基准，更开辟了一种新探针，可用于测量主序星系与星暴星系周围CGM的金属丰度、气体密度及覆盖因子。此外，本次窄带成像联合靶向光谱观测策略的成功，验证了一种可扩展、高性价比的搜寻范式——未来依托鲁宾天文台LSST与Subaru主焦点摄谱仪（PFS）等大型宽视场巡天项目，有望系统性地发现数十个类似天体，从而极大提升我们描绘星系全宇宙尺度重子循环的能力。
