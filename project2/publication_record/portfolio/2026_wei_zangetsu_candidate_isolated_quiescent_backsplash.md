## Short Summary

We report the discovery of Zangetsu—a uniquely isolated, quiescent, and extremely elongated ultra-diffuse galaxy that defies conventional formation models and may be the first confirmed backsplash UDG, opening a new window into the dynamics and evolution of the low-surface-brightness Universe.

**中文：** 我们报告了“Zangetsu”——一个独特孤立、宁静且极度细长的超弥散星系（UDG）的发现。该星系挑战了传统的形成模型，可能是首个被证实的回溅型超弥散星系，为探索低面亮度宇宙的动力学与演化开辟了新窗口。

## Detailed Summary

The discovery of Zangetsu represents a timely and creative leap forward in low-surface-brightness (LSB) galaxy science, addressing a critical observational gap: the scarcity of isolated, quiescent ultra-diffuse galaxies (UDGs) that defy conventional formation paradigms. While recent deep surveys have dramatically expanded the census of UDGs—particularly in dense environments like galaxy clusters—their counterparts in the field remain elusive and poorly understood. Zangetsu fills this void with exceptional clarity: it is not only isolated—lacking any massive neighbor within 1 Mpc—but also exhibits definitive quiescence (g − i = 0.96), indicating an old, passively evolving stellar population inconsistent with ongoing star formation or recent gas accretion. Its extreme photometric properties push the boundaries of known LSB systems, challenging assumptions about how such diffuse, extended structures can form and survive without environmental support or internal feedback mechanisms.

Leveraging the unparalleled depth and resolution of the Hyper Suprime-Cam Subaru Strategic Program (HSC-SSP) imaging—complemented by archival Hubble Space Telescope data—the team employed a multi-faceted approach to characterize Zangetsu. A custom detection pipeline optimized for extreme LSB features identified the object serendipitously in the COSMOS field, followed by rigorous two-dimensional Sérsic modeling yielding an extraordinarily shallow inner profile (n<sub>Sérsic</sub> = 0.40 ± 0.01) and a remarkably low central surface brightness of μ<sub>0,g</sub> = 26.60 ± 0.01 mag arcsec<sup>−2</sup>. Crucially, surface brightness fluctuation (SBF) analysis—applied jointly to HSC and HST data—established a robust lower distance limit of D > 25.4 Mpc, implying a physical effective radius R<sub>e</sub> > 1.38 kpc and placing Zangetsu among the largest known UDGs relative to its luminosity. Its striking morphology—an unusually elongated axis ratio of b/a ∼ 0.25 and mild asymmetry—was quantified via non-parametric shape diagnostics, revealing structural distortions rarely seen in quiescent UDGs.

Zangetsu’s combination of isolation, quiescence, extreme diffuseness, and pronounced elongation makes it a singular object in the current UDG literature. It lies far beyond the established luminosity–size relation for LSB galaxies, defying explanation by standard models—whether tidal disruption, failed Milky Way analogs, or cluster-specific processes like ram-pressure stripping or harassment. Instead, the authors propose a compelling backsplash origin: Zangetsu may have once orbited a massive halo, experienced gravitational heating and star formation quenching during pericenter passage, and subsequently fell back into the field, retaining its diffuse structure and passive evolution. This interpretation elegantly reconciles its isolation with its quiescence and distortion. Beyond Zangetsu itself, the discovery underscores a broader methodological insight—that current UDG searches, often tuned for round, symmetric profiles, may systematically miss highly elongated, low-contrast systems. As such, Zangetsu not only expands the parameter space of viable galaxy formation pathways but also motivates new detection algorithms and deeper, higher-resolution surveys explicitly designed to uncover this hidden population—thereby transforming how we map the faintest, most fragile corners of cosmic structure.

### 中文版

“朧”（Zangetsu）的发现代表了低面亮度（LSB）星系研究领域一次及时而富于创见的重大突破，成功填补了一项关键的观测空白：即孤立、宁静的超弥散星系（UDG）样本严重匮乏——这类星系挑战着现有星系形成理论范式。尽管近期开展的深度巡天已极大扩充了UDG的统计样本，尤其在星系团等高密度环境中成果显著，但其在场星系（field galaxies）中的对应体却依然难以捕捉，且物理性质知之甚少。“朧”以极为清晰的特征填补了这一空白：它不仅处于完全孤立状态（1 Mpc范围内无任何大质量邻近星系），更展现出明确的宁静特性（g − i = 0.96），表明其恒星种群年老且呈被动演化，与持续恒星形成或近期气体吸积过程明显不符。其极端的测光性质进一步拓展了已知LSB星系的参数边界，对传统认知构成挑战——即此类弥散、延展的结构若缺乏环境支撑或内部反馈机制，究竟如何形成并长期维持稳定。

研究团队充分利用超广角主焦点相机 Subaru 战略计划（HSC-SSP）成像数据所具备的无与伦比的深度与分辨率，并辅以哈勃空间望远镜（Hubble Space Telescope）归档数据，采用多维度综合分析方法对“朧”进行了系统表征。一套专为探测极端LSB结构优化的定制化源检测流程，在COSMOS天区中偶然识别出该目标；随后通过严格的二维Sérsic模型拟合，获得其异常平缓的内区剖面（n<sub>Sérsic</sub> = 0.40 ± 0.01）以及极低的中心面亮度（μ<sub>0,g</sub> = 26.60 ± 0.01 mag arcsec<sup>−2</sup>）。尤为关键的是，研究团队联合运用HSC与HST数据开展面亮度涨落（SBF）分析，确立了其稳健的距离下限（D > 25.4 Mpc），由此推得其物理有效半径 R<sub>e</sub> > 1.38 kpc，使其成为目前已知同等光度下尺度最大的UDG之一。其显著的形态特征——异常拉长的轴比（b/a ∼ 0.25）及微弱的不对称性——则借助非参数化形状诊断方法予以量化，揭示出在宁静UDG中罕见的结构畸变。

“朧”集孤立性、宁静性、极端弥散性与显著拉长形态于一体，在当前UDG文献中堪称独一无二。它远远偏离已建立的LSB星系光度–尺寸关系，无法由标准模型（如潮汐瓦解、失败的银河系类似体，或星系团特有的冲压剥离、星系骚扰等过程）予以解释。相反，作者提出一种颇具说服力的“回溅”（backsplash）起源假说：“朧”可能曾围绕某一巨大暗晕运行，在近心点附近经历引力加热与恒星形成熄灭，随后回落至场星系区域，从而保留了其弥散结构与被动演化状态。该物理解释巧妙地调和了其孤立性、宁静性与结构畸变三者之间的内在关联。除“朧”本身外，此项发现还蕴含更广泛的方法论启示：当前多数UDG搜寻策略往往针对圆形、对称轮廓进行优化，因而可能系统性遗漏高度拉长、对比度极低的星系系统。因此，“朧”不仅拓展了星系形成路径的可行参数空间，更推动研发新型探测算法，并激励开展更深、更高分辨率的巡天项目——专门致力于揭示这一隐匿星系群体，从而彻底革新我们对宇宙中最暗弱、最脆弱结构角落的测绘方式。
