## Short Summary

MaNGA’s integral-field survey reveals that 11% of nearby brightest cluster galaxies host multiple stellar cores—a direct observational signature of ongoing cannibalism—matching IllustrisTNG simulations and finally reconciling observed BCG mass growth rates with theory over the past 4.5 billion years.

**中文：** MaNGA 的积分场巡天揭示，附近最亮团星系（BCG）中有 11% 拥有多个恒星核——这是正在进行的星系并合“同类相食”过程的直接观测证据。该结果与 IllustrisTNG 数值模拟高度一致，最终在近 45 亿年的时间尺度上，使观测得到的 BCG 质量增长速率与理论预言达成统一。

## Detailed Summary

This study tackles a persistent puzzle in galaxy evolution: why the observed late-time mass growth of brightest cluster galaxies (BCGs)—the most massive, gravitationally dominant systems in the universe—appears significantly slower than predicted by leading cosmological simulations. While hierarchical assembly dictates that BCGs should continue growing via mergers well into the present epoch, direct observational constraints on *ongoing* merger activity have remained scarce and indirect. Hsu et al. address this gap with an elegant, observationally grounded approach: rather than relying on broad photometric or kinematic signatures prone to degeneracies, they directly identify and quantify *multiple stellar cores* within BCGs—unambiguous morphological tracers of recent or ongoing minor and intermediate-mass mergers. This strategy provides a clean, physically motivated proxy for the central merger rate, enabling a precise, apples-to-apples comparison with simulated predictions and moving beyond integrated properties to probe the actual accretion events shaping BCGs today.

Leveraging the unparalleled spatially resolved spectroscopic power of SDSS-IV MaNGA, the team analyzed a carefully selected sample of 79 BCGs at redshifts 0.06–0.15—spanning the last ~4.5 Gyr of cosmic time—with high signal-to-noise integral field data covering physical scales down to ~1 kpc. They combined deep, multi-band imaging with MaNGA’s stellar kinematics and stellar population maps to robustly distinguish true co-spatial multiple cores from line-of-sight projections or tidal features. Crucially, they validated their identification methodology using mock MaNGA observations generated from the state-of-the-art IllustrisTNG cosmological hydrodynamical simulation, applying identical analysis pipelines to both real and synthetic data. This rigorous, simulation-calibrated framework ensures that detection biases, resolution limits, and selection effects are consistently accounted for—transforming a simple morphological census into a quantitative, physically interpretable measurement of merger frequency.

The results are striking in their clarity and concordance: the observed multiple-core fraction is 0.11 ± 0.04 within an 18 kpc radius—a statistically significant detection confirming that ongoing cannibalism is not rare but a regular feature of BCG evolution. Remarkably, this value aligns closely with the 0.08 ± 0.04 fraction recovered from the IllustrisTNG mock observations, providing strong empirical validation of the simulation’s treatment of late-stage BCG assembly. Moreover, the study reveals that the majority of candidate companion cores identified in imaging are indeed physically associated systems—not chance alignments—underscoring the fidelity of their joint photometric–spectroscopic approach. These findings resolve the long-standing tension between models and observations by demonstrating that BCG mass growth over the past 4.5 Gyr proceeds at a rate fully consistent with ΛCDM expectations, driven predominantly by dry mergers whose signatures are now unambiguously captured. By forging a direct, observationally anchored link between core multiplicity and merger-driven growth, this work establishes a new benchmark for testing galaxy formation physics and opens a powerful pathway for probing hierarchical assembly across cosmic time with next-generation IFU surveys.

### 中文版

本研究致力于解决星系演化领域一个长期存在的难题：为何观测到的最亮团星系（BCGs）——宇宙中质量最大、引力主导性最强的天体系统——在晚近时期的质增速率，显著低于当前主流宇宙学数值模拟所预言的结果。尽管等级成团理论明确指出，BCGs应通过并合过程持续增长直至当今时代，但针对其*正在进行中*的并合活动，直接的观测约束却始终稀缺且多为间接证据。Hsu 等人以一种精巧而立足于观测的策略弥补了这一空白：他们并未依赖易受简并性干扰的宽波段测光或动力学特征，而是直接识别并量化BCGs内部的*多重恒星核*——这是近期或正在进行的矮质量及中等质量并合事件无可争议的形态学示踪体。该方法提供了一个清晰、物理动机明确的中心并合率代理量，从而实现与模拟预测之间严格“同类对比”（apples-to-apples）的精确检验，并突破积分性质的局限，直接探测量子尺度上正在塑造当今BCGs的实际吸积事件。

依托SDSS-IV MaNGA前所未有的空间分辨光谱能力，研究团队分析了一个精心筛选的79个BCGs样本，红移范围为0.06–0.15，覆盖了宇宙演化史上最近约4.5 Gyr的时间跨度；所用MaNGA积分视场数据信噪比高，物理空间分辨率可达约1 kpc。研究人员将深度多波段成像与MaNGA提供的恒星动力学图及恒星种群分布图相结合，稳健地区分出真正共空间的多重核结构与视线方向投影或潮汐特征。尤为关键的是，他们利用当前最先进的宇宙学流体动力学模拟IllustrisTNG生成了MaNGA风格的模拟观测数据，并对真实数据与合成数据采用完全一致的分析流程，从而对检测偏差、分辨率极限及选择效应进行了严格校准。这一严谨的、经模拟标定的分析框架，成功将一项简单的形态学普查转化为对并合频率的定量、物理解释明确的测量。

结果清晰而富有说服力：在18 kpc半径内，观测到的多重核比例为0.11 ± 0.04——这一具有统计显著性的探测明确证实，“持续吞噬”并非罕见现象，而是BCGs演化的常规特征。更引人注目的是，该观测值与IllustrisTNG模拟观测中恢复的0.08 ± 0.04高度吻合，为该模拟对BCGs晚期组装过程的物理刻画提供了强有力的实证支持。此外，研究还揭示：成像中识别出的候选伴星核，绝大多数确为物理关联的真实系统，而非偶然投影，进一步印证了其联合测光–光谱方法的高保真度。这些发现从根本上消解了模型与观测之间长期存在的张力——表明过去4.5 Gyr间BCGs的质量增长速率完全符合ΛCDM宇宙学框架的预期，其主导机制正是“干并合”（dry mergers），而此类并合的独特印记如今已被明确无误地捕捉。通过在核多重性与并合驱动增长之间建立起一条直接、观测锚定的联系，本工作确立了检验星系形成物理的新基准，并为借助下一代积分视场单元（IFU）巡天在全宇宙时标上探测等级成团过程开辟了一条强大路径。
