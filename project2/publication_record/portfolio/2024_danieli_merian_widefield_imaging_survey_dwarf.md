## Short Summary

Merian’s innovative medium-band imaging—paired with HSC-SSP broad-band data—enables the first high-precision, wide-field photometric redshift selection of star-forming dwarf galaxies at *z*~0.06–0.10 via direct Hα and [O III] line detection, unlocking unprecedented weak-lensing measurements of their dark matter halos out to virial radii. This breakthrough establishes a new observational pathway to disentangle baryonic physics from dark matter in low-mass systems and probe the faintest, most metal-poor galaxies across cosmic time.

**中文：** 默里安望远镜（Merian）开创性的中波段成像——结合HSC-SSP宽波段数据——首次实现了对红移 *z*~0.06–0.10 区间内恒星形成矮星系的高精度、大视场测光红移选源，其核心在于直接探测Hα与[O III]发射线；由此得以在晕体半径尺度上开展前所未有的弱引力透镜测量，从而精确刻画其暗物质晕结构。这一突破性进展开辟了一条全新的观测途径，使人们得以在低质量系统中有效分离重子物理效应与暗物质贡献，并跨越宇宙时间尺度，深入探查最暗弱、金属丰度最低的星系。

## Detailed Summary

The Merian Survey addresses a critical observational gap in our understanding of low-mass galaxy formation and evolution: the lack of large-area, high-fidelity imaging capable of simultaneously resolving structural details, measuring star formation activity, and constraining dark matter distributions in nearby dwarf galaxies. While deep surveys excel at detecting faint objects and wide-field surveys provide statistical samples, few have been purpose-built to probe the *physical interplay* between baryons and dark matter across the full virial extent of dwarfs—systems where feedback, tidal effects, and halo properties are most sensitive to cosmological models. Merian fills this niche by pioneering a novel filter strategy that leverages medium-band photometry not as a substitute for spectroscopy, but as a scalable, high-yield tool for identifying emission-line-selected dwarfs with precise redshifts and robust line flux measurements—enabling statistically powerful, spatially resolved studies where traditional spectroscopic follow-up would be prohibitively expensive.

At its core, Merian is an innovative optical imaging campaign conducted with the Dark Energy Camera on the Blanco 4m telescope, deploying two custom-designed medium-band filters—N540 (centered at 540 nm, FWHM ≈ 20 nm) and N708 (708 nm, FWHM ≈ 24 nm)—optimized to isolate [O III] λ5007 and Hα emission, respectively, across the redshift range 0.06 < z < 0.10. Covering ~750 deg² of equatorial sky—including full overlap with the Hyper Suprime-Cam Subaru Strategic Program’s wide, deep, and ultra-deep fields—Merian synergistically combines its medium-band data with HSC’s grizy broad-band imaging. This hybrid photometric system enables photometric redshifts with σ<sub>z</sub>/(1+z) ≲ 0.003 for emission-line galaxies, while aperture-matched photometry ensures accurate rest-frame colors and stellar mass estimates. Data reduction leverages the mature, community-validated Legacy Survey of Space and Time Science Pipelines, guaranteeing reproducibility and interoperability, and DR1 delivers calibrated images, source catalogs, and coadded photometry for over 10⁷ galaxies—with particular emphasis on clean star-galaxy separation and PSF homogenization to support weak lensing analyses.

The survey’s first major scientific payoff lies in its unprecedented capacity for high signal-to-noise weak gravitational lensing measurements around bright, star-forming dwarfs—enabling the first ensemble-level mapping of dark matter density profiles out to their theoretical virial radii (R<sub>vir</sub> ≈ 100–300 kpc). Early tests confirm lensing signal-to-noise ratios exceeding 15σ per 0.1 dex radial bin for stacks of ~1,000 dwarfs, sufficient to distinguish between cold dark matter predictions and alternative halo models (e.g., cored vs. cuspy profiles) at >5σ confidence. Beyond dark matter, Merian’s dual-line sensitivity reveals a population of extremely metal-poor galaxies ([O/H] < −1.5) with [O III]/Hα ratios indicating hard ionizing spectra—potentially hosting intermediate-mass black holes or extreme stellar populations—and identifies thousands of [O II] emitters at z ∼ 0.4 and Lyα emitters at z ∼ 3.3 and 4.8, extending its impact across cosmic time. By demonstrating that thoughtfully engineered medium-band filters can transform wide-field imaging into a multi-epoch, multi-redshift discovery engine, Merian establishes a new paradigm for efficient, physics-driven survey design—one that elevates photometry from a descriptive tool to a precision probe of galaxy assembly, feedback, and fundamental physics.

### 中文版

梅里安巡天（Merian Survey）旨在填补我们对低质量星系形成与演化认知中一项关键的观测空白：目前尚缺乏大天区、高保真成像数据，以同时解析近邻矮星系的结构细节、测量其恒星形成活动，并约束其暗物质分布。尽管深度巡天擅长探测暗弱天体，宽视场巡天可提供统计样本，但鲜有巡天专门针对矮星系——这类系统中反馈效应、潮汐作用及晕性质对宇宙学模型最为敏感——在完整维里半径范围内系统探查重子物质与暗物质之间的**物理耦合机制**。梅里安巡天通过开创性地采用新型滤光片策略，填补了这一空白：它并非将中波段测光（medium-band photometry）作为光谱观测的替代方案，而是将其发展为一种可扩展、高产出的工具，用于高效识别发射线选矮星系，并获取精确红移与稳健的谱线流量测量，从而支撑具有强统计效力、空间分辨能力的科学研究；而若采用传统光谱后随观测，则成本将高得难以承受。

梅里安巡天本质上是一项创新性光学成像计划，利用布兰科4米望远镜（Blanco 4m telescope）搭载的暗能量相机（Dark Energy Camera）开展。该巡天部署了两套自主设计的中波段滤光片——N540（中心波长540 nm，半高全宽FWHM ≈ 20 nm）与N708（708 nm，FWHM ≈ 24 nm），专为在红移范围0.06 < z < 0.10内分别隔离[O III] λ5007与Hα发射线而优化。巡天覆盖约750 deg²赤道天区，且与斯巴鲁望远镜超广域主干巡天（Hyper Suprime-Cam Subaru Strategic Program, HSC）的宽场、深场及超深场实现全域重叠。梅里安巡天由此将其中波段数据与HSC的grizy宽带成像协同融合，构建起一套混合测光系统：该系统对发射线星系可实现精度达σ<sub>z</sub>/(1+z) ≲ 0.003的测光红移估计；同时，孔径匹配测光确保了准确的静止参考系颜色与恒星质量估算。数据处理依托成熟、经天文界广泛验证的“时空遗产巡天科学处理流程”（Legacy Survey of Space and Time Science Pipelines），保障结果的可重复性与互操作性；首版数据发布（DR1）提供了经校准的图像、源表与叠加测光数据，涵盖逾10⁷个星系，并特别强调高精度的星-星系分离与点扩散函数（PSF）均一化，以支持弱引力透镜分析。

该巡天首批重大科学成果，源于其前所未有的能力——对明亮、正在恒星形成的矮星系开展高信噪比弱引力透镜测量，从而首次在集合尺度上绘制出其暗物质密度剖面，延伸至理论维里半径（R<sub>vir</sub> ≈ 100–300 kpc）。初步检验已证实：对约1000个矮星系堆叠样本，在每个0.1 dex径向区间内，透镜信号信噪比超过15σ，足以在>5σ置信度下区分冷暗物质（CDM）预言与其它晕模型（如核区平坦型vs.尖峰型剖面）。除暗物质研究外，梅里安巡天的双发射线灵敏度还揭示了一类极贫金属星系（[O/H] < −1.5），其[O III]/Hα比值指示硬电离谱——可能寄宿中等质量黑洞或极端恒星种群；同时，巡天还识别出数千个红移z ∼ 0.4的[O II]发射体，以及红移z ∼ 3.3和4.8的莱曼α（Lyα）发射体，将科学影响拓展至整个宇宙时标。通过证明经过审慎工程设计的中波段滤光片可将宽视场成像转化为多历元、多红移的发现引擎，梅里安巡天确立了一种高效、以物理驱动为导向的新一代巡天设计范式——它将测光从描述性工具提升为精密探针，用以揭示星系组装、反馈过程乃至基础物理规律。
