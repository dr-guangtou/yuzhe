## Short Summary

UDGs are not failed galaxies or relics of violent feedback—instead, they form stars normally but at uniquely low efficiencies across all scales, challenging models that rely on bursty star formation or stellar expansion to explain their diffuse nature.

**中文：** 超弥散星系（UDG）并非“失败的星系”或剧烈反馈作用的遗迹；相反，它们以正常方式形成恒星，但其恒星形成效率在所有尺度上均显著偏低，这挑战了依赖于爆发式恒星形成或恒星膨胀机制来解释其弥散特性的理论模型。

## Detailed Summary

This study addresses a pivotal tension in modern galaxy evolution: whether ultra-diffuse galaxies (UDGs) represent exotic, dynamically puffed-up systems forged by extreme feedback or instead arise naturally from standard star formation processes operating at unusually low efficiencies. While UDGs have long challenged theoretical models—particularly those invoking rapid stellar expansion driven by bursty, high-efficiency star formation—their star-forming properties had remained largely unexplored at resolved scales, especially within the critical H I-rich regime where gas reservoirs are abundant but star formation is conspicuously subdued. By focusing on 22 H I-selected UDGs within 120 Mpc and contrasting them with a carefully matched sample of 35 low-mass dwarfs from the NASA-Sloan Atlas, this work fills a crucial observational gap: it delivers the first spatially resolved, multi-wavelength characterization of star formation efficiency across kiloparsec to sub-kiloparsec scales in a statistically meaningful UDG sample.

The analysis leverages an innovative, self-consistent joint spectral energy distribution (SED) fitting framework that co-fits optical (Hyper Suprime-Cam Subaru Strategic Program), near-UV (GALEX), and H I data to produce pixel-level maps of star formation rate surface density (ΣSFR) and stellar mass surface density (Σ∗). Crucially, the method incorporates spatially resolved H I radial profiles—where available—from interferometric observations, enabling direct, scale-dependent comparisons of ΣSFR to atomic gas surface density (ΣHI) down to 500 pc resolution. This represents a methodological advance over previous studies relying on global or aperture-based measurements, allowing robust quantification of local star formation physics while mitigating biases from dust attenuation, stellar population age gradients, and blending effects.

The results reveal that UDGs exhibit remarkably low star formation efficiencies—ΣSFR/ΣHI ≈ 10⁻¹⁰ yr⁻¹—consistent across their disks and persisting even at the highest spatial resolutions probed; this is ~10× lower than typical dwarf irregulars at comparable ΣHI. Strikingly, when stellar size is plotted against total H I mass, UDGs fall squarely on the same relation as NSA dwarfs—no offset, no outliers. Their stellar half-light radii are entirely ordinary given their gas content, directly contradicting predictions from “puffing” models that require intense, localized feedback to inflate stellar distributions. Instead, the data strongly favor a picture in which UDGs form stars continuously and quiescently, governed by the same underlying physics as other low-mass galaxies—but operating at systematically suppressed efficiencies, likely due to shallow gravitational potentials and inefficient gas compression. These findings place stringent new constraints on galaxy formation simulations, ruling out models where late-time (z ≲ 0.5), burst-driven feedback is necessary to explain UDG morphology—thereby redirecting theoretical focus toward early assembly histories, environmental preprocessing, and the role of halo spin and accretion geometry in setting long-term star formation ceilings.

### 中文版

本研究聚焦于现代星系演化领域的一个关键矛盾：超弥散星系（UDG）究竟是由极端反馈机制催生的特殊动力学膨胀系统，还是仅由标准恒星形成过程在异常低效条件下自然产生？尽管UDG长期以来对理论模型构成挑战——尤其是那些依赖剧烈、高效率恒星形成所驱动的快速恒星分布膨胀的模型——但其恒星形成性质在分辨尺度上始终缺乏系统性探索，特别是在至关重要的中性氢（H I）丰度区域：该区域内气体储库充沛，恒星形成活动却显著受抑。本工作选取120 Mpc内经H I选源的22个UDG，并与NASA-Sloan星表（NSA）中精心匹配的35个低质量矮星系样本进行对比，填补了一项关键观测空白：首次在具有统计意义的UDG样本中，实现了千秒差距至亚千秒差距尺度上恒星形成效率的空间分辨、多波段表征。

分析采用一种创新的自洽联合能谱能量分布（SED）拟合框架，同步拟合光学波段（Hyper Suprime-Cam Subaru战略计划）、近紫外波段（GALEX）及H I数据，生成像素级的恒星形成率面密度（ΣSFR）与恒星质量面密度（Σ∗）分布图。尤为关键的是，该方法整合了干涉观测提供的空间分辨H I径向轮廓（在可用情况下），从而实现ΣSFR与原子气体面密度（ΣHI）之间直接的、尺度依赖的比对，空间分辨率可达500 pc。相较以往依赖全局或孔径测量的研究，该方法代表了方法论上的重要进步，不仅可稳健量化局地恒星形成物理过程，亦能有效规避尘埃消光、恒星种群年龄梯度及混合效应等系统偏差。

结果表明，UDG展现出极低的恒星形成效率——ΣSFR/ΣHI ≈ 10⁻¹⁰ yr⁻¹，该特征在星系盘内普遍存在，且在所探测的最高空间分辨率下依然保持稳定；其值约为同ΣHI条件下典型矮不规则星系的十分之一。尤为引人注目的是，当以恒星有效半径为纵轴、总H I质量为横轴作图时，UDG与NSA矮星系严格落于同一条关系曲线上——无偏移、无离群体。其恒星有效半径完全符合其气体含量所预期的正常范围，直接否定了“膨胀”模型的预言：该模型要求强烈而局域的反馈作用以显著拉伸恒星分布。相反，观测数据强有力地支持另一种图像：UDG的恒星形成是持续且宁静的，其基本物理规律与其他低质量星系一致，但整体效率系统性偏低，很可能源于浅势阱与气体压缩效率低下。这些发现为星系形成数值模拟施加了新的严格约束，排除了依赖晚近时期（z ≲ 0.5）、爆发式反馈来解释UDG形态的模型，从而将理论研究焦点转向早期并合历史、环境预处理，以及晕角动量与吸积几何结构在设定长期恒星形成上限中所起的关键作用。
