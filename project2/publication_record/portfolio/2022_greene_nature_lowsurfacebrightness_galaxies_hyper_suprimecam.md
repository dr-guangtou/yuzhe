## Short Summary

This study reveals that low-surface-brightness galaxies are not merely faint extensions of normal galaxies but constitute a distinct population—especially above 10⁸ M⊙—whose deviation from the canonical mass-size relation points to unique formation pathways, resolving a long-standing ambiguity in galaxy evolution theory.

**中文：** 本研究揭示，低面亮度星系并非普通星系的微弱延展，而是一个独特的星系群体——尤其在恒星质量高于10⁸ M⊙时——其显著偏离标准的质量-尺寸关系，表明其形成路径具有特殊性，从而厘清了星系演化理论中长期存在的模糊认识。

## Detailed Summary

This study addresses a long-standing observational gap in extragalactic astronomy: the systematic, statistically robust characterization of low-surface-brightness (LSB) galaxies beyond the Local Volume. While LSB systems are predicted to be abundant in hierarchical galaxy formation models—and may hold critical clues about star formation inefficiency, feedback physics, and dark matter halo properties—their faintness has historically hindered large-scale redshift surveys. Prior work relied heavily on small, heterogeneous samples or indirect distance estimates, leaving fundamental questions unanswered about their cosmic distribution, mass–size scaling, and evolutionary distinctness. By leveraging the unprecedented depth, resolution, and survey area of the Hyper Suprime-Cam Strategic Survey Program (HSC-SSP), this work pioneers the first wide-field, magnitude-limited census of LSB galaxies with direct statistical redshift constraints—transforming them from outliers into a quantitatively tractable population.

The analysis combines deep, multi-band HSC imaging over 200 deg² with innovative cross-matching techniques against the NASA–SDSS Atlas, enabling reliable photometric redshift assignments for over 15,000 LSB candidates selected via surface brightness thresholds (μ<sub>g</sub> > 25 mag arcsec⁻²) and structural parameters. Crucially, the team developed a custom Bayesian framework to model selection effects and quantify redshift probability distributions, mitigating biases inherent in traditional stacking or single-redshift assignment methods. This approach—coupled with careful masking of foreground stars, image artifacts, and high-surface-brightness contaminants—ensures that the resulting redshift distribution reflects the true underlying population rather than observational systematics. The methodology thus represents a creative synthesis of modern survey data, archival resources, and probabilistic inference tailored specifically to the challenges of ultra-diffuse objects.

The results reveal a rich and structured cosmic distribution: the majority of LSB galaxies reside within *z* < 0.15 (~500 Mpc), with a pronounced peak at *z* ≈ 0.03 (≤100 Mpc), corresponding to low-mass systems (*M*<sub>*</sub> ≈ 10⁷–10⁸ *M*<sub>⊙</sub>) spanning effective radii of 1–4 kpc—objects that align smoothly with the canonical mass–size relation. Strikingly, a significant tail extends to *z* ≈ 0.15, populated by more massive LSB galaxies (*M*<sub>*</sub> = 10⁸–10⁹ *M*<sub>⊙</sub>) with larger sizes (up to *r*<sub>eff,g</sub> ≈ 8 kpc). Most notably, the data provide the first empirical evidence that above *M*<sub>*</sub> > 10⁸ *M*<sub>⊙</sub>, LSB galaxies systematically deviate from the extrapolated mass–size trend of high-surface-brightness counterparts—suggesting they are not merely “faded” versions of typical dwarfs or disks, but represent a physically distinct channel of galaxy assembly, possibly shaped by early quenching, weak stellar feedback, or unique halo spin histories. These findings establish LSB galaxies as a coherent, observationally accessible probe of galaxy formation under extreme conditions—opening new pathways to test ΛCDM predictions, calibrate baryonic physics in simulations, and refine galaxy evolution frameworks across cosmic time.

### 中文版

本研究填补了星系天文学中一项长期存在的观测空白：对本星系群以外低面亮度（LSB）星系开展系统性、具有统计稳健性的表征。尽管等级式星系形成模型预言LSB星系应大量存在——且其可能蕴含有关恒星形成低效性、反馈物理机制及暗物质晕性质的关键线索——但其固有的微弱亮度却历来阻碍了大样本红移巡天的开展。此前工作多依赖于小规模、异质性强的样本，或仅能提供间接的距离估计，致使关于这类星系的宇宙学分布、质量–尺寸标度关系及其演化独特性等基本问题始终悬而未决。本研究充分利用Hyper Suprime-Cam战略巡天计划（HSC-SSP）所具备的前所未有的深度、分辨率与巡天面积，首次实现了对LSB星系的大视场、星等限制普查，并为其赋予直接的统计红移约束，从而将LSB星系从边缘异常体转变为可定量研究的星系种群。

本分析整合了覆盖200 deg²天区的HSC多波段深度成像数据，并创新性地采用交叉证认技术，与NASA–SDSS星图（NASA–SDSS Atlas）进行匹配，从而为逾15,000个LSB候选体（依据面亮度阈值μ<sub>g</sub> > 25 mag arcsec⁻²及结构参数遴选）提供了可靠的测光红移赋值。尤为关键的是，研究团队构建了一套定制化的贝叶斯框架，用以建模选择效应并量化红移概率分布，有效规避了传统堆叠法或单红移赋值方法所固有的系统偏差。该方法结合对前景恒星、图像伪迹及高面亮度污染源的精细掩模处理，确保所得红移分布真实反映LSB星系本征总体，而非观测系统误差所致。因此，本方法论体现了现代巡天数据、历史档案资源与概率推断技术的创造性融合，专为应对超弥散天体这一特殊挑战而量身定制。

研究结果揭示出一个丰富且结构清晰的宇宙学分布：绝大多数LSB星系位于*z* < 0.15（约500 Mpc）范围内，其中在*z* ≈ 0.03（≤100 Mpc）处呈现显著峰值，对应低质量系统（*M*<sub>*</sub> ≈ 10⁷–10⁸ *M*<sub>⊙</sub>），有效半径介于1–4 kpc之间——此类天体与标准的质量–尺寸关系平滑衔接。尤为引人注目的是，分布存在一条显著延伸至*z* ≈ 0.15的长尾，由质量更大（*M*<sub>*</sub> = 10⁸–10⁹ *M*<sub>⊙</sub>）、尺寸更广（*r*<sub>eff,g</sub>最高达≈8 kpc）的LSB星系构成。最具突破性的是，数据首次提供了经验性证据：当恒星质量超过*M*<sub>*</sub> > 10⁸ *M*<sub>⊙</sub>时，LSB星系系统性偏离高面亮度星系外推所得的质量–尺寸趋势——表明它们并非典型矮星系或盘状星系的简单“褪色”版本，而是代表一种物理上截然不同的星系组装通道，其形成或受早期熄火、微弱恒星反馈或特殊的暗物质晕角动量历史所主导。这些发现确立了LSB星系作为一类自洽、可观测的探针，可用于深入探究极端条件下的星系形成过程，从而开辟新路径以检验ΛCDM模型预言、校准数值模拟中的重子物理、并在宇宙时标上完善星系演化理论框架。
