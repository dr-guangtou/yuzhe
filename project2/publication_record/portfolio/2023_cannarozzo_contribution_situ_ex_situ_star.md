## Short Summary

IllustrisTNG’s remarkable agreement with MaNGA observations—especially in stellar mass, metallicity, and age profiles—provides the strongest observational validation to date that massive early-type galaxies form predominantly through merger-driven evolution, where major mergers homogenize stellar populations and erase progenitor signatures.

**中文：** IllustrisTNG 模拟结果与 MaNGA 观测数据——特别是在恒星质量、金属丰度和恒星年龄径向分布方面——展现出卓越的一致性，为迄今最强有力的观测证据，表明大质量早型星系主要通过并合驱动的演化形成：其中主并合事件使恒星成分趋于均一，并抹除其前身星系的特征印记。

## Detailed Summary

This study addresses a pivotal question in modern galaxy evolution: how do the competing pathways of *in situ* (internally formed) and *ex situ* (accreted via mergers) star formation jointly sculpt the structural, chemical, and dynamical properties of massive early-type galaxies (ETGs) in the local Universe? While theoretical models have long predicted that mergers dominate the growth of the most massive ETGs, robust observational constraints on the radial distribution and relative contributions of these two stellar populations have remained elusive—particularly beyond integrated or aperture-limited measurements. By bridging high-fidelity integral-field spectroscopy with state-of-the-art cosmological hydrodynamics, this work fills a critical gap by delivering the first spatially resolved, quantitative comparison of *in situ* versus *ex situ* contributions across multiple physical dimensions—mass surface density, metallicity, age, and kinematics—in a statistically robust sample of real and simulated galaxies.

The authors leverage the MaNGA survey’s unprecedented spatially resolved spectroscopic data for over 1,000 massive ($M_* \geq 10^{10.5}\,M_\odot$) ETGs, extracting robust radial profiles of stellar mass surface density ($\Sigma_*$), stellar metallicity ([Z/H]), stellar age, and line-of-sight velocity dispersion ($\sigma$). These are compared directly to matched mock observations from the TNG100 simulation—the highest-resolution run of the IllustrisTNG suite—where each stellar particle is tagged as either *in situ* or *ex situ* based on its formation history. Crucially, the analysis goes beyond global matching: it decomposes the simulated profiles into their constituent formation channels and maps how each contributes to the observed MaNGA trends, enabling a physically grounded interpretation of the data rather than mere phenomenological fitting.

The results reveal remarkable fidelity between MaNGA and TNG100 across nearly all diagnostics: stellar mass surface density profiles agree in both shape and normalization to within $\sim5\%$, while metallicity and age gradients match in slope and amplitude, and velocity dispersion distributions align closely for central and satellite galaxies alike. Even the subtle differences—such as the $\sim50\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$ excess in $\sigma$ for the most massive centrals ($M_* \gtrsim 10^{11.5}\,M_\odot$) in TNG100—prove instructive, pointing to specific aspects of merger dynamics and black hole feedback that can now be targeted for refinement. Most compellingly, the decomposition shows that *ex situ* stars dominate the outer halos ($R \gtrsim 2\,R_e$) of high-mass ETGs, contributing up to $70\%$ of the stellar mass beyond $4\,R_e$, while *in situ* stars prevail in the inner regions—yet the merger-driven homogenization is so effective that the resulting composite profiles reproduce MaNGA observations with striking precision. This provides direct, spatially resolved evidence that major mergers are not just incidental but *architectural*: they erase memory of progenitor diversity and forge dynamically relaxed, chemically coherent systems whose observable properties emerge naturally from hierarchical assembly. The work thus establishes a new benchmark for testing galaxy formation physics, demonstrating that modern simulations can now quantitatively reproduce the multi-dimensional anatomy of real galaxies—and offering a powerful framework to probe the cosmic interplay between gravity, gas physics, and stellar feedback across cosmic time.

### 中文版

本研究聚焦于现代星系演化领域的一个核心问题：原位（内部形成）与异地（通过并合吸积）恒星形成这两种竞争性路径，如何共同塑造本地宇宙中大质量早型星系（ETGs）的结构、化学及动力学性质？尽管理论模型长期预言，并合过程主导着最重ETGs的增长，但针对这两类恒星成分在径向分布及其相对贡献方面的稳健观测约束始终匮乏——尤其在超越积分光度或孔径限制测量的尺度上。本工作通过将高保真积分视场光谱学与前沿宇宙学流体动力学模拟相融合，填补了这一关键空白，首次在真实星系与模拟星系的统计学可靠样本中，实现了原位与异地恒星成分在多个物理维度（面质量密度、金属丰度、恒星年龄及运动学）上的空间分辨、定量对比。

作者充分利用MaNGA巡天所提供的、迄今最完备的空间分辨光谱数据，对逾1000个大质量（$M_* \geq 10^{10.5}\,M_\odot$）ETGs提取出稳健的径向剖面，涵盖恒星面质量密度（$\Sigma_*$）、恒星金属丰度（[Z/H]）、恒星年龄以及视线方向速度弥散（$\sigma$）。这些观测剖面被直接与IllustrisTNG模拟套件中最高分辨率运行TNG100所生成的匹配模拟观测进行比对；在该模拟中，每个恒星粒子均依据其形成历史被明确标记为“原位”或“异地”成分。尤为关键的是，本分析超越了全局匹配层面：它将模拟剖面按形成通道进行分解，并系统映射各成分对MaNGA观测趋势的具体贡献，从而实现对数据的物理解释，而非仅停留于经验拟合。

结果表明，MaNGA观测与TNG100模拟在几乎所有诊断指标上均展现出惊人的一致性：恒星面质量密度剖面在形状与归一化水平上吻合度达$\sim5\%$；金属丰度与年龄梯度在斜率与振幅上高度一致；中心星系与卫星星系的速度弥散分布亦紧密吻合。即便是细微差异——例如TNG100中质量最大的中心星系（$M_* \gtrsim 10^{11.5}\,M_\odot$）其$\sigma$值存在约$50\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$的系统性偏高——也具有重要启示意义，精准指向了并合动力学与黑洞反馈等特定物理过程，为后续模型优化提供了明确靶点。最具说服力的是，成分分解揭示：在高质ETGs中，“异地”恒星主导其外围晕区（$R \gtrsim 2\,R_e$），在$4\,R_e$以外区域可贡献高达70%的恒星质量；而“原位”恒星则主导内区。然而，并合驱动的均质化效应极为高效，由此形成的复合剖面以惊人的精度复现了MaNGA观测结果。这为并合事件不仅是偶发过程、更是星系“架构性”塑造力量提供了直接的、空间分辨的观测证据：它们抹除了子系多样性记忆，锻造出动力学弛豫、化学自洽的系统，其可观测性质自然涌现于等级式并合组装过程之中。本工作由此确立了检验星系形成物理的新基准，证实现代数值模拟已能定量重现真实星系的多维结构特征，并为深入探究引力、气体物理与恒星反馈在宇宙时标上的协同演化提供了一个强大而普适的分析框架。
