## Short Summary

We find that low surface brightness galaxies are intrinsically oblate spheroids—regardless of color or environment—and their observed thickness strongly favors formation via outward migration of star formation over alternative pathways, providing the first robust, observationally grounded constraint on LSBG genesis.

**中文：** 我们发现，低面亮度星系本质上均为扁椭球体——无论其颜色或所处环境如何——且其观测到的厚度特征强烈支持恒星形成向外迁移的形成机制，而非其他可能的形成途径；这为低面亮度星系的起源提供了首个坚实、基于观测的约束。

## Detailed Summary

This study addresses a long-standing and critical gap in our understanding of low surface brightness galaxies (LSBGs): their intrinsic three-dimensional structure. While LSBGs have emerged as key probes of galaxy formation in the faint, gas-rich, and dark-matter-dominated regime, their physical origins remain hotly debated—ranging from failed Milky Way satellites to “failed” giant galaxies, or even products of radial migration or tidal stirring. Crucially, prior shape analyses either ignored selection biases inherent to surface-brightness-limited surveys or conflated projection effects with true structural diversity, leaving the intrinsic shape distribution poorly constrained and its implications for formation mechanisms ambiguous. By directly confronting these observational biases head-on, this work establishes the first robust, self-consistent inference of the intrinsic shape ensemble for LSBGs across multiple independent, large-area surveys—thereby transforming shape from a qualitative curiosity into a quantitative discriminant of formation physics.

The authors pioneered a novel forward-modeling framework that jointly fits the observed distributions of projected ellipticity *and* apparent surface brightness for over 21,000 LSBGs drawn from three complementary datasets: the Hyper Suprime-Cam Subaru Strategic Program (781 galaxies), the Dark Energy Survey (20,977), and the Legacy Survey (188 H I–selected systems). Critically, the model explicitly incorporates the survey-specific surface brightness limits and detection thresholds as integral parts of the likelihood function—ensuring that inferred intrinsic shapes are not artifacts of selection but reflect the underlying population. This approach leverages both optical morphology and surface brightness sensitivity in a unified statistical framework, augmented by rigorous Monte Carlo testing and consistency checks across survey methodologies and redshift ranges (z < 0.3). The analysis further distinguishes red and blue LSBGs and benchmarks results against high surface brightness dwarf galaxies and simulated analogues from state-of-the-art cosmological hydrodynamical simulations—including IllustrisTNG, EAGLE, and FIRE-2.

The results reveal that LSBGs are overwhelmingly well described as oblate spheroids, with a median intrinsic axial ratio (c/a) of 0.56 ± 0.03 and a dispersion in flattening consistent with a log-normal distribution centered on q ≈ 0.55. Remarkably, no statistically significant difference is found between red and blue LSBGs—suggesting that star formation history does not drive major differences in global shape, contrary to expectations if quenching were strongly tied to environmental compression or ram-pressure stripping. Moreover, LSBGs are measurably thicker (i.e., less flattened) than comparably massive high surface brightness dwarfs, whose median c/a is ~0.45—a distinction that points to fundamentally different dynamical assembly histories. Most compellingly, the observed shape distribution aligns closely with predictions from simulations where LSBGs form via *in-situ* radial migration of star formation to large radii, while models relying predominantly on tidal disruption or early collapse produce systematically flatter or more triaxial configurations inconsistent with the data.

These findings represent a conceptual leap forward: for the first time, intrinsic shape is used not merely as a descriptive statistic but as a decisive, observationally grounded constraint on galaxy formation pathways. The agreement with radial migration scenarios underscores the importance of internal secular processes—even in the lowest-density regimes—and challenges narratives that prioritize external environmental triggers for LSBG genesis. By anchoring theoretical models to a robust, bias-corrected observable, this work provides a new benchmark for next-generation simulations and informs the design of future deep imaging and IFU surveys targeting LSB structures. Ultimately, it affirms that LSBGs are not exotic outliers but natural outcomes of well-understood baryonic physics operating under extreme surface brightness conditions—opening a fresh, geometrically informed window into how galaxies build their outer stellar halos across cosmic time.

### 中文版

本研究致力于解决低面亮度星系（LSBGs）研究中一个长期存在且至关重要的科学空白：其本征三维结构。尽管LSBGs已成为探索暗物质主导、气体丰沛、光度微弱等极端物理环境下星系形成机制的关键探针，但其物理起源仍存在激烈争论——观点涵盖“失败的银河系卫星星系”、“夭折的巨椭圆星系”，乃至径向迁移或潮汐搅拌的产物。尤为关键的是，以往关于星系形状的分析，或忽视了面亮度受限巡天固有的选择效应，或将投影效应与真实的结构多样性混为一谈，致使LSBGs本征形状分布长期缺乏可靠约束，其对形成机制的物理解读亦因而模糊不清。本工作直面上述观测偏差，首次在多个独立大天区巡天数据基础上，建立了稳健、自洽的LSBGs本征形状系综推断框架，从而将星系形状这一参数，从定性描述提升为定量判别星系形成物理过程的关键诊断工具。

作者开创性地构建了一套新颖的前向建模框架，联合拟合来自三组互补数据集的逾21,000个LSBGs的投影椭率与表观面亮度观测分布：Hyper Suprime-Cam Subaru Strategic Program（781个星系）、Dark Energy Survey（20,977个）以及Legacy Survey（188个H I选源系统）。该模型的核心创新在于，明确将各巡天特有的面亮度极限与探测阈值作为似然函数的内在组成部分，从而确保所推断出的本征形状并非选择效应的人为产物，而是真实反映底层星系总体的统计特性。该方法在统一的统计框架下协同利用光学形态信息与面亮度灵敏度，并辅以严格的蒙特卡洛检验及跨巡天方法学与红移范围（z < 0.3）的一致性验证。分析进一步区分了红序与蓝序LSBGs，并将结果与高面亮度矮星系及当前最先进的宇宙学流体动力学模拟（包括IllustrisTNG、EAGLE与FIRE-2）中的类LSBGs模拟样本进行基准比对。

结果表明，LSBGs绝大多数可被优异描述为扁椭球体，其中位本征轴比（c/a）为0.56 ± 0.03，其压扁程度弥散符合以q ≈ 0.55为中心的对数正态分布。尤为引人注目的是，红序与蓝序LSBGs之间未发现具有统计显著性的形状差异——这暗示恒星形成历史并未导致其整体形状发生重大分化，从而与“环境压缩或冲压剥离主导星系熄灭”这一预期相悖。此外，LSBGs明显比质量相当的高面亮度矮星系更厚（即压扁程度更低），后者中位c/a约为0.45；这一显著区别指向二者在动力学组装历史上的根本性差异。最具说服力的是，观测所得的形状分布与如下模拟预言高度吻合：LSBGs主要通过恒星形成的*原位*径向迁移延伸至大半径处而形成；而以潮汐瓦解或早期坍缩为主导的模型则系统性地预言更扁平或更三轴的构型，与观测数据不符。

这些发现标志着概念层面的重大跃进：本征形状首次不再仅作为描述性统计量，而是成为一种决定性、且严格基于观测的星系形成路径约束。其与径向迁移情景的高度一致，凸显了内禀缓变过程（secular processes）即使在宇宙最低密度环境中亦具关键作用，同时挑战了将外部环境触发机制视作LSBGs成因主导因素的传统叙事。本工作通过将理论模型锚定于一个经系统偏差校正的可观测量，不仅为下一代宇宙学模拟树立了新的基准，也为未来面向低面亮度结构的深度成像与积分场光谱（IFU）巡天的设计提供了重要指导。最终，该研究确证：LSBGs并非奇异的离群体，而是标准重子物理在极端面亮度条件下自然演化的产物——由此开启了一扇崭新的、以几何视角为驱动的窗口，用以探究星系如何在宇宙时标上构筑其外围恒星晕。
