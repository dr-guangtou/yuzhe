## Short Summary

This study reveals that ultra-puffy galaxies—mass-size outliers around Milky Way analogs—are quenched at the same rate as normal-sized satellites, proving that extreme size alone does not drive environmental quenching; instead, it’s host color and proximity that matter, while the classic UDG definition artificially conflates size with quiescence.

**中文：** 本研究揭示，围绕银河系类似体的“超蓬松星系”（即质量-尺寸关系中的异常体）与正常尺寸的卫星星系具有相同的熄灭率，从而证明极端尺寸本身并非环境致熄灭的驱动因素；真正起决定性作用的是宿主星系的颜色和距离，而传统的超漫延矮星系（UDG）定义则人为地将尺寸与宁静态混为一谈。

## Detailed Summary

This study addresses a critical and timely gap in our understanding of galaxy evolution: the interplay between structural anomalies—specifically, galaxies that are anomalously large for their stellar mass—and environmental quenching mechanisms. While ultra-diffuse galaxies (UDGs) have dominated recent discourse on “puffy” dwarfs, their definition is intrinsically biased toward low surface brightness *and* red, quiescent systems, obscuring whether size outliers themselves are preferentially quenched or merely co-located with quenched populations. By shifting focus to *mass-size outliers*—a physically motivated, mass-selected sample—we decouple structural peculiarity from star formation state, enabling a clean test of whether environmental processes like ram pressure stripping or tidal heating preferentially affect galaxies with inflated stellar distributions. This conceptual reframing is central to the work’s novelty and directly responds to growing calls in the field for more robust, theory-aligned definitions of extreme dwarf populations.

Leveraging the unprecedented depth (r ∼ 26.5 mag), resolution, and sky coverage of the Hyper Suprime-Cam Strategic Survey (HSC-SSP), the team identified 147 ultra-puffy galaxies (UPGs) around 1,283 Milky Way–analog hosts (M⋆ ≈ 10¹⁰.⁵–¹¹.⁵ M⊙) out to 300 kpc projected radius. UPGs were rigorously defined as satellites lying ≥1.5σ above the median size–mass relation for dwarfs (log Rₑ [kpc] = 0.56 log M⋆ [M⊙] − 5.2), ensuring objectivity and reproducibility. Structural parameters were measured via multi-band Sérsic modeling with careful PSF deconvolution and background subtraction; host properties (color, mass, morphology) were drawn from the same HSC-SSP photometry and cross-matched spectroscopic catalogs. Crucially, the analysis was validated against the IllustrisTNG simulation suite, where synthetic UPGs were selected using identical observational criteria—enabling direct, apples-to-apples comparison between data and theory.

The results reveal a striking and counterintuitive insight: UPGs exhibit a quenched fraction of 68 ± 5%—statistically indistinguishable from that of normal-sized satellites (66 ± 3%) around the same hosts. This null result powerfully demonstrates that being a mass-size outlier *per se* does not predispose a dwarf to environmental quenching. Instead, quenching correlates strongly with host properties and orbital proximity: UPGs near redder, more massive hosts show elevated quenching (up to 82 ± 7%), and those within 100 kpc projected radius are quenched at twice the rate of those beyond 200 kpc. In contrast, classical UDGs—selected by surface brightness alone—show markedly higher quenching (89 ± 4%) and systematically redder colors, confirming they represent a highly biased subset dominated by already-quenched systems. These findings establish that the UDG classification conflates structural and evolutionary signatures, whereas the UPG framework cleanly isolates size anomalies as a probe of pre-quenching dynamical history.

This work fundamentally advances the field by redefining how we interpret structural outliers in galaxy evolution studies. It provides the first robust, observationally grounded evidence that environmental quenching operates independently of a galaxy’s current size–mass offset—suggesting that puffiness may instead trace early assembly history, angular momentum retention, or transient expansion phases rather than imminent gas removal. The tight integration of deep imaging, mass-based selection, and simulation validation sets a new methodological standard for environmental studies of dwarfs. For future surveys like LSST and Euclid, this framework offers a scalable, physics-driven pathway to disentangle the roles of internal dynamics, feedback, and environment in shaping the faint end of the galaxy population—making it not only a significant conceptual leap but also a practical roadmap for next-generation cosmological investigations.

### 中文版

本研究聚焦于星系演化领域一个关键且亟待解决的科学问题：结构异常星系（即恒星质量相同但有效半径显著偏大的星系）与环境致钝机制之间的关联。尽管超弥散星系（UDG）近年来成为“蓬松型”矮星系研究的焦点，但其定义本身固有地偏向于低面亮度且呈红色、宁静态的系统，从而掩盖了一个根本性问题——尺寸离群体本身是否更易被致钝，抑或仅是与已致钝星系在空间上共存？本工作将研究视角转向“质量–尺寸离群体”（mass-size outliers）——一种基于物理动机、以恒星质量为选择依据的样本——从而在概念上解耦结构异常性与恒星形成状态，进而开展一项干净、直接的检验：诸如冲压剥离或潮汐加热等环境过程，是否优先作用于恒星分布膨胀的星系。这一概念重构构成了本研究的核心创新点，并直接回应了学界日益强烈的呼吁——亟需建立更稳健、更契合理论预期的极端矮星系定义体系。

依托Hyper Suprime-Cam战略巡天（HSC-SSP）前所未有的深度（r ∼ 26.5 mag）、分辨率与天区覆盖能力，研究团队在300 kpc投影半径内、围绕1283个银河系类比宿主星系（M⋆ ≈ 10¹⁰.⁵–¹¹.⁵ M⊙）成功识别出147个超蓬松星系（UPG）。UPG被严格定义为：作为卫星星系，其有效半径位于矮星系尺寸–质量关系中位线之上≥1.5σ处（log Rₑ [kpc] = 0.56 log M⋆ [M⊙] − 5.2），确保了定义的客观性与可重复性。结构参数通过多波段Sérsic模型拟合获得，并辅以精细的点扩散函数（PSF）反卷积与背景扣除；宿主星系属性（颜色、质量、形态）则统一源自HSC-SSP测光数据及交叉匹配的光谱星表。尤为关键的是，本分析经由IllustrisTNG数值模拟套件进行了系统验证：在模拟中采用完全相同的观测标准选取合成UPG样本，从而实现了观测数据与理论模型之间真正意义上的“同类对比”。

研究结果揭示了一项引人注目的反直觉发现：UPG的致钝比例为68 ± 5%，在统计上与同一宿主周围正常尺寸卫星星系的致钝比例（66 ± 3%）无显著差异。这一“零结果”强有力地表明，单纯作为质量–尺寸离群体本身，并不增加矮星系遭受环境致钝的倾向。相反，致钝现象与宿主星系性质及轨道距离强相关：靠近更红、更高质量宿主的UPG表现出更高的致钝率（最高达82 ± 7%）；而投影距离在100 kpc以内的UPG，其致钝率则是200 kpc以外者的两倍。相比之下，仅依据面亮度选取的经典UDG则展现出明显更高的致钝比例（89 ± 4%）及系统性更红的颜色，证实其本质上是一个高度偏差的子样本，主导成员均为早已致钝的星系。这些发现确立了UDG分类法实质上混淆了结构特征与演化印记，而UPG框架则能清晰分离尺寸异常，使之成为探测致钝前动力学历史的理想探针。

本工作从根本上推动了该领域的认知范式演进，重新定义了我们在星系演化研究中解读结构离群体的方式。它首次提供了坚实、基于观测的证据，表明环境致钝过程独立于星系当前的尺寸–质量偏离程度；这意味着“蓬松性”更可能反映的是早期并合历史、角动量保留效应或瞬时膨胀相，而非即将发生的气体剥离。深度成像、质量驱动的选择策略与模拟验证的高度整合，为矮星系环境研究树立了新的方法论标杆。对于LSST与Euclid等未来巡天项目而言，该框架提供了一条可扩展、具物理内涵的路径，用以厘清内部动力学、反馈过程与环境效应在塑造星系光度函数暗端所扮演的角色——这不仅是一次重大的概念跃迁，更是一份面向下一代宇宙学探索的切实可行路线图。
