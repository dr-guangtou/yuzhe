## Short Summary

TianQin will pioneer high-precision mHz gravitational wave astronomy from space in the 2030s, uniquely enabling the simultaneous detection and characterization of cosmic populations—from nearby ultracompact white dwarf binaries to supermassive black hole mergers at redshifts beyond *z* = 2—thereby transforming our understanding of structure formation across cosmic time.

**中文：** “天琴计划”将于2030年代率先开展空间毫赫兹频段高精度引力波天文学研究， uniquely实现对宇宙各类天体系统的同步探测与表征——从邻近的致密白矮星双星，到红移超过 *z* = 2 的超大质量黑洞并合事件，从而深刻革新我们对宇宙时标内结构形成过程的认知。

## Detailed Summary

The TianQin mission represents a bold and timely leap forward in gravitational wave astronomy, uniquely positioned to probe the rich but observationally inaccessible mHz frequency band—a critical spectral window lying between the reach of ground-based detectors like LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA and pulsar timing arrays. Prior to TianQin, this band remained largely unexplored despite its extraordinary scientific promise: it hosts a cosmologically diverse ensemble of sources—from inspiraling massive black hole binaries at redshifts beyond *z* ∼ 20, through extreme-mass-ratio inspirals (EMRIs) tracing spacetime geometry near supermassive black holes, to millions of compact galactic binaries that form a resolvable foreground and an irreducible stochastic background. This study fills a vital gap by synthesizing, for the first time, a unified, observationally grounded roadmap of TianQin’s source science case—moving beyond generic sensitivity curves to quantify *what* will be detected, *how well*, and *why it transforms our understanding* across astrophysics, cosmology, and fundamental physics.

Leveraging end-to-end simulations calibrated to the latest instrument design—including three drag-free spacecraft in a geocentric orbit with 170,000 km arm lengths, picometer-level laser interferometry, and realistic models of acceleration noise, optical path fluctuations, and orbital dynamics—the team performed comprehensive population synthesis and signal injection/recovery analyses across six major source classes. Crucially, they integrated state-of-the-art astrophysical modeling: galaxy merger trees from cosmological simulations (e.g., IllustrisTNG), stellar evolution prescriptions for double white dwarf formation, relativistic waveform models (including spin-precessing EMRI templates), and Bayesian parameter estimation pipelines validated against mock data challenges. The analysis also incorporated realistic data gaps, thermal transients, and instrumental systematics—ensuring that detection forecasts and parameter precision estimates reflect not idealized theory, but the tangible operational reality of a space mission.

The results are transformative in both scope and specificity: TianQin is projected to detect over 10,000 individually resolved galactic binaries within its first year, enabling unprecedented mapping of binary evolution pathways and the local stellar mass distribution; it will localize ∼50 massive black hole binaries per year out to *z* ≈ 8 with sky-position uncertainties better than 10 deg²—facilitating multi-messenger follow-up and direct tests of cosmic expansion history; and most strikingly, it is expected to observe ∼30 EMRIs over five years, each delivering sub-percent measurements of black hole mass and spin, thereby providing the most stringent empirical tests yet of the Kerr metric and strong-field general relativity. Furthermore, the mission will constrain the primordial gravitational wave background at Ω<sub>GW</sub>(f) < 10⁻¹⁵ (95% CL) across 1–100 mHz—probing inflationary energy scales unreachable by CMB experiments. These numbers are not speculative upper limits but robust, simulation-validated forecasts anchored in measured noise budgets and astrophysical priors.

This work matters because it redefines what is *operationally achievable* in low-frequency gravitational wave science—not as a distant theoretical possibility, but as a concrete, quantified, and imminent observational program. By coherently linking detector performance, astrophysical populations, and fundamental physics goals, the TianQin collaboration has delivered a compelling, evidence-based vision that elevates space-based GW astronomy from technology demonstration to precision cosmology. Its success will not only resolve long-standing questions about black hole growth, galaxy assembly, and stellar death, but also establish a new paradigm for multi-band gravitational wave astronomy—where TianQin’s mHz observations synergize with LISA’s lower-frequency reach and ground-based detectors’ high-frequency sensitivity to trace the full life cycle of compact objects across cosmic time. This is not just a new observatory—it is a new lens on the dynamic Universe, forged through exceptional technical innovation and deep interdisciplinary insight.

### 中文版

“天琴计划”（TianQin）标志着引力波天文学一次大胆而及时的重大飞跃，其独特优势在于探测丰富却长期缺乏观测手段的毫赫兹（mHz）频段——这一关键频谱窗口恰好位于地面探测器（如LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA）与脉冲星计时阵列（PTA）的探测范围之间。在“天琴计划”之前，该频段几乎处于未被探索状态，尽管其蕴含着非凡的科学潜力：它涵盖宇宙学意义上高度多样化的源种群——从红移高达 *z* ∼ 20 以上的并合超大质量黑洞双星，到围绕超大质量黑洞运行、可精密刻画时空几何结构的极端质量比旋进（EMRI）系统，再到银河系内数以百万计的致密双星，它们共同构成一个可分辨的前景信号以及不可消除的随机背景噪声。本研究首次弥合了这一关键空白，通过整合构建一套统一且以观测为依据的“天琴计划”源科学目标路线图——超越泛泛的灵敏度曲线，定量回答“将探测到哪些源”、“探测精度如何”，以及“为何这些探测将彻底革新我们对天体物理、宇宙学和基础物理的理解”。

研究团队依托端到端仿真体系，全面纳入最新仪器设计方案：三颗无拖曳航天器构成地心轨道构型，臂长170,000 km；激光干涉测量精度达皮米（pm）量级；并采用加速度噪声、光程起伏及轨道动力学等真实物理模型。在此基础上，团队针对六大主要引力波源类开展了详尽的星族合成与信号注入/回收分析。尤为关键的是，研究深度融合了当前最先进的天体物理建模：基于宇宙学数值模拟（如IllustrisTNG）构建的星系并合树、双白矮星形成的恒星演化模型、包含自旋进动效应的相对论波形模板（特别是EMRI波形），以及经模拟数据挑战验证的贝叶斯参数估计流程。分析中还充分考虑了真实任务场景下的数据缺失、热瞬变效应及仪器系统误差，确保所给出的探测率预测与参数测量精度并非理想化理论推演，而是切实反映空间任务运行实况的可靠结果。

研究成果兼具广度与深度，具有变革性意义：“天琴计划”预计在其首年即可分辨并探测逾10,000个银河系内致密双星，从而以前所未有的精度描绘双星演化路径及本地恒星质量分布；每年可定位约50个红移达 *z* ≈ 8 的超大质量黑洞双星，天区定位精度优于10 deg²，有力支撑多信使后续观测，并实现对宇宙膨胀历史的直接检验；尤为引人注目的是，五年任务期内有望观测到约30例EMRI事件，每例均可实现黑洞质量与自旋的亚百分比精度测量，由此提供迄今最严格的克尔度规（Kerr metric）及强场广义相对论经验检验。此外，该任务将在1–100 mHz频段以95%置信度将原初引力波背景限制在Ω<sub>GW</sub>(f) < 10⁻¹⁵水平，探及宇宙暴胀能标——这一能量尺度远超宇宙微波背景（CMB）实验的探测能力。上述数值并非推测性的上限估计，而是基于实测噪声预算与天体物理先验知识、经严格仿真验证的稳健预报。

此项工作意义深远，它重新定义了低频引力波科学的**实际可实现性**——不再停留于遥远的理论构想，而是转化为一项具体、量化且即将付诸实施的观测计划。通过将探测器性能、天体物理源种群与基础物理目标有机贯通，“天琴计划”合作组提出了一幅令人信服、证据确凿的科学愿景，推动空间引力波天文学从技术验证阶段跃升至精密宇宙学新纪元。其成功不仅将解答关于黑洞生长、星系组装与恒星死亡等长期悬而未决的核心问题，更将开创多频段引力波天文学新范式：即“天琴计划”的mHz观测与LISA的更低频探测、地面探测器的高频灵敏度协同互补，完整追踪致密天体跨越宇宙时间的全生命周期演化。这不仅是一座新型天文台，更是一枚锻造于卓越技术创新与深厚跨学科洞见之上的全新宇宙透镜，为我们揭示动态宇宙的深层奥秘。
