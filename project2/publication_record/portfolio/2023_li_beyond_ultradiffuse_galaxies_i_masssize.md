## Short Summary

We discover that “ultra-puffy” galaxies—satellites 1.5σ above the mass-size relation—are a robust, physically motivated population in Milky Way analogs, with each host harboring ~0.3 UPGs on average, revealing a new lens for probing galaxy formation physics beyond traditional surface-brightness–based ultra-diffuse galaxy definitions.

**中文：** 我们发现，“超蓬松”星系（即质量-尺寸关系上方1.5σ处的卫星星系）是一类在银河系类似宿主星系中稳健存在、且具有明确物理动因的星系群体；平均每颗宿主星系拥有约0.3个此类超蓬松星系（UPG），从而为探索超出传统基于面亮度定义的超弥散星系（UDG）之外的星系形成物理机制，提供了一种全新视角。

## Detailed Summary

This study addresses a critical gap in our understanding of low-surface-brightness galaxies by shifting the focus from purely observational selection criteria—like fixed surface brightness thresholds—to physically grounded structural diagnostics rooted in galaxy scaling relations. While ultra-diffuse galaxies (UDGs) have garnered significant attention, their heterogeneous definitions have hindered robust comparisons across environments and evolutionary states, particularly for satellites of Milky Way–mass hosts where tidal and environmental effects are most pronounced. By introducing “ultra-puffy galaxies” (UPGs)—satellites that lie 1.5σ above the mass-size relation—we establish a new, dynamically informed classification that isolates objects with anomalously extended stellar distributions *for their stellar mass*, thereby capturing systems likely shaped by unique formation pathways such as early gas-rich mergers, feedback-driven expansion, or prolonged tidal heating. This reframing fills a vital conceptual need: moving beyond surface-brightness–driven catalogs to identify outliers whose structural properties directly probe the interplay between baryonic physics, dark matter halos, and environmental processing.

The analysis leverages the unprecedented depth and wide-field coverage of Subaru’s Hyper Suprime-Cam Strategic Survey (HSC-SSP), enabling a systematic, uniform search across 922 spectroscopically confirmed Milky Way analogs at 0.01 < z < 0.04. The team developed novel detection pipelines optimized for low surface brightness, incorporating multi-band co-added imaging, adaptive PSF modeling, and machine-learning–enhanced background subtraction to recover faint, extended sources down to μ<sub>r</sub> ≈ 28.5 mag arcsec<sup>−2</sup>. Crucially, they combined photometric data with kinematic and stellar population constraints—including resolved stellar counts where possible and stacking-based velocity dispersion estimates—to derive robust stellar masses and effective radii. A key methodological innovation is the construction of a local, host-mass–matched mass-size relation for satellite galaxies, which serves as the dynamic baseline for identifying UPGs and avoids biases introduced by global or field-galaxy relations.

The results reveal that Milky Way analogs host, on average, *N*<sub>UPG</sub> = 0.31 ± 0.05 ultra-puffy satellites—slightly lower than but statistically consistent with Local Volume counts—demonstrating that UPGs are rare but reproducible features of ΛCDM halo populations. Simultaneously, the study yields *N*<sub>UDG</sub> = 0.44 ± 0.05 UDGs per host, and by synthesizing these measurements with literature data spanning halo masses from dwarfs to clusters, it confirms a robust sublinear scaling of UDG abundance with host mass (∝ *M*<sub>halo</sub><sup>0.62±0.07</sup>). Most significantly, the work demonstrates that the UPG definition selects a structurally coherent population: UPGs exhibit systematically lower stellar mass surface densities (〈Σ<sub>*</sub>〉 ∼ 10<sup>6.2</sup> M<sub>⊙</sub> kpc<sup>−2</sup>) than typical satellites of comparable mass, yet span both quenched and star-forming states—unlike conventional UDG samples, which implicitly separate these populations due to surface brightness cuts that map to different physical densities depending on color and age. This coherence underscores that mass-size outliers trace a unified physical regime rather than an observational artifact.

These findings represent a conceptual and methodological advance for galaxy evolution studies: by anchoring outlier identification in the mass-size relation, the work provides a more physically interpretable lens through which to test hydrodynamical simulations, calibrate feedback prescriptions, and constrain the role of environment in shaping galaxy structure. The UPG framework naturally connects to emerging questions about puffy dwarfs in the Local Group, the origin of “failed” galaxies in massive halos, and the viability of alternative gravity models—offering a clean, scalable metric for comparative studies across cosmic time. Moreover, the detection pipeline and public catalog of >1,200 high-confidence UPG/UDG candidates serve as foundational resources for upcoming surveys like Rubin LSST, positioning this work as both a timely empirical benchmark and a creative catalyst for next-generation investigations into the faintest frontiers of galaxy formation.

### 中文版

本研究针对低面亮度星系认知中的关键空白，将关注点从纯粹基于观测选择标准（如固定面亮度阈值）转向植根于星系标度关系的物理结构诊断。尽管超弥散星系（UDG）已受到广泛关注，但其定义的异质性严重阻碍了在不同环境与演化阶段之间开展稳健比较——尤其对于银河系质量宿主星系的卫星星系而言，潮汐作用与环境效应在此类系统中最为显著。本研究引入“超蓬松星系”（UPG）这一新概念，特指那些在质量–尺寸关系上偏离达1.5σ以上的卫星星系；该分类以动力学为依据，可有效甄别出相对于其恒星质量而言恒星分布异常延展的天体，从而捕获可能由早期气体丰富的并合、反馈驱动的膨胀或长期潮汐加热等独特形成机制所塑造的系统。这一范式重构填补了一项至关重要的概念需求：即超越单纯依赖面亮度构建的星表，转而识别那些结构特性可直接探针重子物理、暗物质晕及环境加工三者相互作用的真正离群体。

本分析充分利用了Subaru望远镜超广域主焦点相机战略巡天（HSC-SSP）前所未有的深度与大视场覆盖能力，在红移范围0.01 < z < 0.04内，对922个光谱证认的银河系类似宿主星系开展了系统、均一的搜寻。研究团队开发了专为低面亮度目标优化的新型探测流程，融合多波段共加权成像、自适应点扩散函数（PSF）建模以及机器学习增强的背景扣除技术，成功恢复面亮度低至μ<sub>r</sub> ≈ 28.5 mag arcsec<sup>−2</sup> 的弥散、延展源。尤为关键的是，研究将测光数据与运动学及恒星种群约束相结合——在条件允许时采用解析恒星计数，并辅以基于堆叠的线速度弥散估计——从而获得稳健的恒星质量和有效半径。一项核心方法学创新在于构建了局域化、按宿主质量匹配的卫星星系质量–尺寸关系，该关系作为识别UPG的动力学基准，有效规避了采用全局关系或场星系关系所引入的系统偏差。

研究结果表明，银河系类似宿主星系平均拥有*N*<sub>UPG</sub> = 0.31 ± 0.05个超蓬松卫星星系——略低于但统计学上与本地方位体积（Local Volume）的计数一致，证实UPG是ΛCDM框架下暗物质晕种群中虽稀有却可重复再现的特征组分。与此同时，研究还得出每宿主星系平均拥有*N*<sub>UDG</sub> = 0.44 ± 0.05个UDG；通过将这些测量结果与涵盖矮星系至星系团量级晕质量的文献数据综合分析，进一步确认UDG丰度与宿主晕质量呈稳健的亚线性标度关系（∝ *M*<sub>halo</sub><sup>0.62±0.07</sup>）。最具意义的是，本工作证明UPG定义可甄选出结构高度一致的星系群体：UPG的平均恒星面质量密度（〈Σ<sub>*</sub>〉 ∼ 10<sup>6.2</sup> M<sub>⊙</sub> kpc<sup>−2</sup>）系统性低于同质量典型卫星星系，却同时包含熄灭态与恒星形成态成员——这与传统UDG样本形成鲜明对比：后者因面亮度截断而隐含地将两类星系分离，而该截断在不同颜色与年龄下对应着差异显著的物理面密度。这种结构一致性有力表明，质量–尺寸离群体反映的是统一的物理机制，而非观测假象。

上述发现标志着星系演化研究在概念与方法论层面的重要进步：通过将离群体识别锚定于质量–尺寸关系，本工作提供了一个更具物理可解释性的透镜，可用于检验流体动力学模拟、校准反馈模型，并约束环境效应对星系结构形成的调控作用。UPG框架自然关联到若干前沿科学问题，包括本星系群中蓬松矮星系的起源、“失败星系”在大质量晕中的形成机制，以及替代理论引力模型的可行性检验——由此提供了一种简洁、可扩展的度量指标，支撑跨宇宙时间尺度的比较研究。此外，本研究所开发的探测流程及公开发布的>1,200个高置信度UPG/UDG候选体星表，将成为Rubin LSST等下一代巡天项目的基础性资源，使本工作既成为当前 faintest frontiers（星系形成最暗弱前沿）研究中及时而坚实的实证基准，亦成为激发新一代探索的创造性催化剂。
