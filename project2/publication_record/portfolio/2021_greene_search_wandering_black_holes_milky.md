## Short Summary

This first-of-its-kind search for wandering intermediate-mass black holes in the Milky Way—using Gaia’s precision astrometry and DECaLS’ deep imaging to hunt for their telltale hypercompact star clusters—sets the strongest observational limits to date, ruling out 100% occupancy and paving the way for next-generation surveys to finally detect these elusive cosmic nomads.

**中文：** 这是首次在银河系内搜寻游荡型中等质量黑洞的研究——利用盖亚（Gaia）卫星的高精度天体测量数据与暗能量巡天（DECaLS）的深度成像，搜寻其特征性超致密星团。该研究迄今给出了最强的观测限制，完全排除了此类黑洞100%占据的可能性，为下一代巡天项目最终探测到这些神秘的宇宙游牧者铺平了道路。

## Detailed Summary

This study addresses a compelling and long-standing gap in our understanding of black hole demographics in the Milky Way: the elusive population of wandering intermediate-mass black holes (IMBHs), which may have been stripped from dwarf galaxy satellites during hierarchical galaxy assembly. While supermassive black holes anchor galactic nuclei and stellar-mass black holes are increasingly detected via gravitational waves, IMBHs—bridging the mass gap between these extremes—remain observationally enigmatic. Their detection is critical for testing theories of black hole seeding, growth, and dynamical evolution in low-mass systems, yet no unambiguous, dynamically confirmed IMBH has been identified in the Galactic halo. Greene et al. (2021) pioneered a novel, multi-wavelength strategy that shifts the search paradigm from direct accretion signatures—which are faint and transient—to the persistent, kinematically coherent stellar systems they may gravitationally bind: hypercompact star clusters (HCCs) with radii ~1 pc and stellar populations of 500–5000 stars.

The team executed a creative, data-driven approach by cross-matching high-precision astrometry from Gaia DR2 with deep, wide-field optical imaging from the Dark Energy Camera Legacy Survey (DECaLS). They developed tailored selection criteria to identify compact, unresolved stellar overdensities with small proper motion dispersions—hallmarks of bound systems orbiting unseen massive perturbers—and applied rigorous statistical background modeling to distinguish genuine candidates from chance alignments or image artifacts. Crucially, they anchored their search sensitivity in realistic theoretical expectations using the semi-analytic satellite evolution model SatGen, which simulates the infall history of dwarf galaxies into the Milky Way and predicts that up to ~100 wandering IMBHs (with masses 10³–10⁵ M⊙) could reside in the halo if *every* infalling satellite hosted a central black hole. This forward-modeling framework allowed them to translate non-detections into physically meaningful upper limits on the IMBH occupation fraction.

Although no definitive HCC candidates were identified across the surveyed sky area (~5,000 deg²), this null result is highly informative: it robustly rules out 100% IMBH occupation in dwarf progenitors, marking the first observational constraint of its kind on the prevalence of “black hole fossils” in the Galactic halo. More importantly, the study establishes a powerful new methodology—one that synergizes precision astrometry with deep photometry to probe dark massive objects through their stellar kinematic imprints—and identifies key avenues for future progress. The authors emphasize that improved constraints on HCC structural parameters (e.g., size distribution, luminosity function, and remnant star fractions) will dramatically sharpen sensitivity in upcoming surveys like Gaia DR4 and Rubin LSST. By transforming an absence of detections into a foundation for next-generation searches, this work exemplifies how innovative data fusion and theoretically grounded survey design can advance frontier questions in black hole astrophysics.

### 中文版

本研究聚焦于银河系黑洞种群统计学中一个引人入胜且长期悬而未决的关键问题：游荡型中等质量黑洞（IMBHs）——这类天体可能在等级式星系并合过程中，从矮星系卫星系统中被剥离而出，因而迄今仍难以捉摸。尽管超大质量黑洞稳居星系核区中心，而恒星级质量黑洞正通过引力波日益频繁地被探测到，但作为连接这两类极端质量黑洞之间关键质量间隙的IMBHs，其观测证据依然匮乏。对IMBHs的可靠探测对于检验黑洞种子形成、生长机制以及低质量系统中动力学演化理论至关重要；然而，迄今为止，尚未在银河系晕中发现任何经动力学确证、无可争议的IMBH。Greene等人（2021）开创性地提出一种新颖的多波段搜寻策略，将探测范式由依赖微弱且暂现的直接吸积辐射信号，转向搜寻IMBHs可能通过引力束缚形成的、具有持久性与运动学相干性的恒星系统——即尺度约1 pc、包含500–5000颗恒星的超致密星团（HCCs）。

研究团队采用了一种富有创意且以数据驱动的方法：将盖亚第二期数据发布（Gaia DR2）提供的高精度天体测量数据，与暗能量相机遗产巡天（DECaLS）获取的深度宽视场光学成像数据进行交叉比对。他们构建了专门设计的筛选准则，用于识别那些角尺度紧凑、无法分辨、且自行弥散度极小的恒星过密区域——此类特征正是受不可见大质量扰动源引力束缚的致密系统的典型标志；同时，他们应用严谨的统计背景建模方法，以有效区分真实候选体与随机投影叠加或图像伪迹。尤为关键的是，该团队基于半解析卫星演化模型SatGen，将搜寻灵敏度锚定于现实的理论预期之上：SatGen模拟了矮星系向银河系并合的吸积历史，并预测——若每个并合矮星系均曾拥有中心黑洞，则银河系晕中可能存有高达约100个游荡型IMBH（质量范围为10³–10⁵ M⊙）。这一前向建模框架使研究者得以将“未探测到”的结果转化为具有明确物理意义的IMBH占据率上限。

尽管在所覆盖的天区（约5,000 deg²）内未发现确凿无疑的HCC候选体，但这一零结果极具科学价值：它稳健地排除了矮星系前身天体中IMBH占据率为100%的可能性，从而首次为银河系晕中“黑洞化石”的普遍性提供了观测约束。更重要的是，本研究建立了一套强大而崭新的方法论——通过精密天体测量与深度测光的协同融合，借助恒星运动学印记来探查不可见的大质量天体——并明确了未来取得突破的关键路径。作者特别指出，对HCC结构参数（如尺度分布、光度函数及残余恒星占比）认知的进一步提升，将在盖亚第四期数据发布（Gaia DR4）和鲁宾时空遗产巡天（Rubin LSST）等下一代巡天项目中显著增强探测灵敏度。本工作通过将“未探测到”转化为下一代搜寻工作的坚实基础，充分彰显了创新性数据融合与理论驱动型巡天设计如何共同推动黑洞天体物理学前沿问题的深入探索。
