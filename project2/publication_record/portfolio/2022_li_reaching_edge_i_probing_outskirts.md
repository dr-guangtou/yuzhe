## Short Summary

This study establishes DECaLS as a robust, wide-field alternative to HSC for measuring outer stellar light in massive galaxies—demonstrating sub-0.05 dex agreement in enclosed stellar mass and equivalent low-scatter correlation with halo mass via weak lensing—thereby unlocking cluster-finding potential across the full DECaLS footprint.

**中文：** 本研究确立了DECaLS作为测量大质量星系外围恒星光度的可靠、宽视场替代方案，其围成恒星质量与HSC结果的偏差小于0.05 dex，并通过弱引力透镜效应展现出与晕质量高度一致、弥散度极低的相关性，从而充分释放DECaLS全天区覆盖范围内的星系团搜寻潜力。

## Detailed Summary

This study addresses a critical and timely gap in extragalactic astrophysics: the need for robust, cross-validated measurements of stellar light at the extreme outskirts of massive galaxies—regions where faint stellar haloes encode vital information about galaxy assembly history and dark matter halo properties. While theoretical work has long predicted that outer light should trace halo mass with low scatter, observational progress has been hampered by systematic uncertainties in sky subtraction, scattered light, and calibration across wide-field surveys. Prior studies often relied on single datasets with unquantified systematics at surface brightness levels fainter than μ<sub>r</sub> ≈ 27 mag arcsec<sup>−2</sup>, leaving open questions about measurement reproducibility and physical interpretability. By systematically comparing four independent imaging surveys—HSC, DECaLS, SDSS, and Dragonfly—this work establishes the first comprehensive benchmark for outer-light photometry, transforming a methodological challenge into an opportunity for synergy across facilities.

The team developed a unified analysis framework to extract azimuthally averaged surface brightness profiles for over 200 massive early-type galaxies spanning redshifts 0.01 < z < 0.50. They implemented custom sky-subtraction pipelines tailored to each survey’s unique noise structure and optical artifacts—including novel corrections for HSC’s large-scale background gradients and DECaLS’s scattered light from bright stars—and validated them using deep coadds, blank-sky regions, and forward-modeling simulations. Crucially, they leveraged Dragonfly’s ultra-low-noise, low-scatter optics as a “gold standard” for nearby systems (z < 0.05), achieving unprecedented depth of μ<sub>r</sub> ≈ 30 mag arcsec<sup>−2</sup>. For intermediate-redshift samples, they demonstrated that HSC reliably recovers individual profiles to μ<sub>r</sub> ≈ 28.5 mag arcsec<sup>−2</sup> out to projected semimajor axes of 100–150 kpc, while DECaLS delivers statistically consistent results beyond 200 kpc—despite its coarser resolution—enabling ensemble-level science across vast cosmological volumes.

The findings deliver several concrete, high-impact advances: stellar mass measurements within 100 kpc agree between DECaLS and HSC to within 0.05 dex—a remarkable level of precision for such faint, extended emission—while weak lensing analyses confirm that outer-light proxies derived from both DECaLS and HSC exhibit similarly tight correlations with halo mass (scatter σ[log M<sub>halo</sub>] < 0.15 dex) in the 0.19 < z < 0.50 range. Most significantly, this work demonstrates that DECam-based imaging—already covering over 10,000 deg<sup>2</sup> in DECaLS and soon to expand further with Rubin LSST—can now be deployed not just for galaxy surveys, but as a *precision halo-mass estimator* for massive systems. This transforms wide-field optical data from a passive mapping tool into an active probe of cosmic structure formation, opening new pathways for cluster finding, halo occupation modeling, and tests of ΛCDM predictions on galactic scales—all grounded in empirically validated, multi-survey consistency.

### 中文版

本研究致力于解决星系天文学中一个关键且亟待突破的难题：在大质量星系极端外围区域开展稳健、交叉验证的恒星光度测量——这些微弱恒星晕区域蕴含着关于星系并合历史及暗物质晕性质的重要信息。尽管理论模型长期预言，外围光度分布可低弥散地示踪暗物质晕质量，但观测进展却一直受限于宽视场巡天中天光扣除、散射光及定标等方面的系统性不确定性。以往研究多依赖单一数据集，在面亮度低于μ<sub>r</sub> ≈ 27 mag arcsec<sup>−2</sup>时缺乏对系统误差的量化评估，致使测量结果的可重复性与物理可解释性仍存疑问。本工作通过系统比对四项独立成像巡天——HSC、DECaLS、SDSS与Dragonfly，首次构建了外围光度测光的综合性基准，将方法学挑战转化为跨设备协同增效的新机遇。

研究团队开发了一套统一分析框架，针对红移范围0.01 < z < 0.50内的200余个大质量早型星系，提取其方位角平均面亮度轮廓。该框架为各巡天独特的噪声特性与光学伪迹定制了天光扣除流程——包括针对HSC大尺度背景梯度及DECaLS亮星散射光的新型校正算法，并利用深度拼接图像、空白天区及前向建模模拟对其进行了严格验证。尤为关键的是，团队充分利用Dragonfly望远镜超低噪声、低散射的光学系统，将其作为近邻星系（z < 0.05）的“金标准”，实现了前所未有的探测深度——μ<sub>r</sub> ≈ 30 mag arcsec<sup>−2</sup>。对于中红移样本，研究证实HSC可在投影长半轴达100–150 kpc范围内可靠恢复单个星系轮廓至μ<sub>r</sub> ≈ 28.5 mag arcsec<sup>−2</sup>；而分辨率较粗的DECaLS则在超过200 kpc处仍给出统计上一致的结果，从而支持在广阔宇宙学体积内开展集合统计研究。

本研究取得若干具体且高影响力的重要进展：在100 kpc半径内，DECaLS与HSC所得恒星质量测量结果相互吻合，偏差不超过0.05 dex——对于如此微弱、延展的辐射而言，这一精度实属卓越；弱引力透镜分析进一步表明，在0.19 < z < 0.50红移区间，由DECaLS与HSC共同导出的外围光度示踪量均与晕质量呈现高度紧密的相关性（对数晕质量弥散σ[log M<sub>halo</sub>] < 0.15 dex）。最具突破性的是，本工作证实：基于DECam的成像数据——目前已通过DECaLS覆盖逾10,000 deg<sup>2</sup>天区，未来更将随Rubin LSST大幅扩展——不仅可用于常规星系普查，更可作为大质量星系的*精密晕质量估测器*。此举将宽视场光学数据从被动的天图绘制工具，升华为主动探测宇宙结构形成的有力探针，为星系团搜寻、晕占据建模以及ΛCDM模型在星系尺度上的检验开辟全新路径——所有结论均建立在经多巡天实证检验的一致性基础之上。
