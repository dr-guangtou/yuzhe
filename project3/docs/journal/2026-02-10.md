## Lessons learned
- Module name collisions across projects are insidious: project1's `config.py` and project3's both get found on sys.path. Fixed by renaming project3's to `p3_config.py`.
- A&A (EDP Sciences) stores abstracts in `<meta name="citation_abstract">` with HTML-encoded content, not in body `<em>` tags as initially assumed. Always inspect actual HTML before writing parsers.
