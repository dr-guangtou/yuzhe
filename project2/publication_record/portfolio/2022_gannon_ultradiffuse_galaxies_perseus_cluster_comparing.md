## Short Summary

This study reveals that GC-rich ultra-diffuse galaxies in the Perseus cluster possess significantly higher stellar velocity dispersions—and thus greater dynamical masses within their half-light radii—than their GC-poor counterparts, providing direct observational evidence that GC richness traces underlying halo mass differences among UDGs.

**中文：** 本研究揭示，英仙座星系团中球状星团（GC）丰富的超弥散星系（UDG）具有显著更高的恒星速度弥散度，因而其半光半径内的动力学质量也明显更大；这为球状星团丰度可示踪超弥散星系间暗晕质量差异这一观点提供了直接的观测证据。

## Detailed Summary

This study addresses a pivotal open question in galaxy evolution: whether the striking diversity in globular cluster (GC) system richness among ultra-diffuse galaxies (UDGs) reflects fundamentally different formation pathways—particularly divergent roles of environment, halo mass, and quenching history. While UDGs have long challenged conventional models of galaxy scaling relations, the recent discovery of GC-rich UDGs with dozens to hundreds of clusters—comparable to massive ellipticals—has intensified debate over whether such systems are “failed giants” or “puffed-up dwarfs.” Prior work lacked statistically robust, homogeneous measurements linking GC content to *dynamical* properties within a single, well-defined cluster environment; this gap hindered tests of competing theories, especially those invoking early versus late environmental quenching or halo-mass–driven GC formation efficiency. By focusing on the Perseus cluster—a dynamically mature, X-ray bright system at intermediate redshift (z ≈ 0.018)—this work delivers the first targeted, high-fidelity dynamical characterization of a cleanly selected, mixed GC-population UDG sample, enabling direct empirical discrimination between these scenarios.

The team leveraged a powerful multi-wavelength observational strategy: deep, wide-field Subaru/Hyper Suprime-Cam imaging to identify and structurally characterize five spectroscopically confirmed UDGs—including three GC-poor (N_GC < 10) and two GC-rich (N_GC > 30) systems—and then obtained high-sensitivity, integral-field spectroscopy with Keck’s Cosmic Web Imager (KCWI) to measure stellar kinematics. KCWI’s exceptional spatial resolution and throughput enabled precise extraction of velocity dispersion profiles within the half-light radius (R_e ≈ 2–4 kpc) for all five targets, even at their low surface brightness (μ₀,g ≈ 25–27 mag arcsec⁻²). Crucially, recessional velocities confirmed unambiguous membership in the Perseus cluster (v_helio ≈ 5,400 km s⁻¹), eliminating projection effects that plague field UDG studies. The analysis combined forward-modeling of KCWI data cubes with Bayesian inference to derive robust σₑ values, which were then converted to dynamical masses M_dyn(<R_e) using spherical Jeans modeling—accounting for anisotropy degeneracies via literature-informed priors. These new measurements were placed in context using a curated compilation of published UDG kinematics and GC counts from Coma, Virgo, and Fornax.

The results reveal a clear, physically significant trend: GC-rich UDGs exhibit systematically higher velocity dispersions—σₑ = 32 ± 4 km s⁻¹ and 36 ± 5 km s⁻¹ for the two rich systems—compared to GC-poor UDGs, whose dispersions range from 14 ± 3 to 21 ± 3 km s⁻¹. This translates to dynamical masses within R_e that are 2.5–4× greater for GC-rich versus GC-poor UDGs (M_dyn(<R_e) ≈ 1.2–2.0 × 10⁹ M⊙ vs. 0.3–0.8 × 10⁹ M⊙). Notably, one GC-poor UDG, PUDG_R24, shows spectroscopic signatures of ongoing quenching—elevated [OII] equivalent width and suppressed Hα—suggesting it is transitioning into the UDG regime within ~0.5 Gyr, offering a rare real-time glimpse into UDG formation. Critically, the lack of correlation between GC richness and phase-space position (e.g., clustercentric radius or infall velocity) rules out models where GC-rich UDGs are exclusively pre-processed satellites quenched at high redshift in group environments before cluster infall. Instead, the tight σₑ–N_GC relation strongly supports a primary role for halo mass: GC-rich UDGs reside in more massive dark matter halos (M_halo ∼ 10¹¹–10¹² M⊙), likely enabling both efficient early GC formation and sustained star formation histories that preserve larger stellar velocity dispersions.

These findings represent a conceptual advance in understanding galaxy quenching and structural evolution across mass scales. By establishing a direct, observationally grounded link between GC system richness and dynamical mass in UDGs—free from the biases of photometric proxies or heterogeneous datasets—the study transforms GC counts from a descriptive curiosity into a quantitative diagnostic of halo potential. This reframes UDGs not as a monolithic class defined solely by surface brightness, but as a diverse population whose internal dynamics encode their assembly history: GC-rich systems are genuine low-surface-brightness analogs of massive galaxies, while GC-poor UDGs align more closely with tidally stripped or environmentally quenched dwarfs. For theory, the results place stringent constraints on hydrodynamical simulations—demanding accurate reproduction of both GC formation efficiency *and* stellar kinematics in low-density regimes. For future surveys, they validate GC richness as a powerful, observationally efficient tracer of halo mass in resolved low-luminosity systems, paving the way for leveraging next-generation facilities like Rubin LSST and JWST to map the dark matter–baryon connection across cosmic time.

### 中文版

本研究聚焦于星系演化领域一个关键的未解之谜：超弥散星系（UDG）球状星团（GC）系统丰度所呈现的显著多样性，是否反映了其根本不同的形成路径——特别是环境、暗晕质量及熄灭历史所起的差异化作用。尽管UDG长期以来挑战着星系尺度关系的传统模型，但近期发现了一批拥有数十至数百个球状星团的GC丰度型UDG，其GC数量堪比大质量椭圆星系，这进一步激化了学界关于此类系统究竟属于“失败的巨星级星系”还是“膨胀的矮星系”的争论。此前研究受限于缺乏在单一、明确定义的星系团环境中，对GC含量与*动力学*性质之间开展具有统计稳健性且观测一致性的测量；这一空白严重制约了对各类理论模型（尤其是涉及早期或晚期环境致熄灭机制、以及暗晕质量主导的GC形成效率模型）的检验能力。本工作以英仙座星系团（Perseus cluster）为研究目标——这是一个动力学已充分弛豫、X射线辐射明亮、红移居中（z ≈ 0.018）的典型富星系团——首次针对一组经严格筛选、兼具GC贫乏与GC丰度两类成员的UDG样本，开展了靶向性高保真动力学表征，从而实现了对上述竞争性形成图景的直接经验判别。

研究团队采用了一套强大的多波段观测策略：首先利用Subaru/Hyper Suprime-Cam开展深度宽视场成像，识别并结构表征了五例光谱证认的UDG，其中包括三例GC贫乏系统（N_GC < 10）与两例GC丰度系统（N_GC > 30）；继而使用凯克望远镜的宇宙网成像仪（KCWI）获取高灵敏度积分视场光谱，以测量恒星运动学。KCWI卓越的空间分辨率与光通量优势，使得研究团队得以在全部五个目标的半光半径（R_e ≈ 2–4 kpc）内精确提取速度弥散剖面，即使其面亮度极低（μ₀,g ≈ 25–27 mag arcsec⁻²）。尤为关键的是，退行速度测量确认所有目标均明确隶属于英仙座星系团（v_helio ≈ 5,400 km s⁻¹），彻底排除了场星系UDG研究中难以规避的投影效应干扰。数据分析结合KCWI数据立方体的前向建模与贝叶斯推断，导出了稳健的σₑ值；再通过球对称Jeans模型将其转化为R_e内的动力学质量M_dyn(<R_e)，并在建模中依据文献先验信息对速度各向异性简并性予以合理约束。这些新获得的动力学测量结果，被置于一个精心汇编的现有UDG运动学与GC计数数据集框架下进行比对分析，涵盖后发座（Coma）、室女座（Virgo）与天炉座（Fornax）等主要星系团。

研究结果揭示出一条清晰且具物理意义的趋势：GC丰度型UDG展现出系统性更高的速度弥散——两个GC丰度系统的σₑ分别为32 ± 4 km s⁻¹与36 ± 5 km s⁻¹；而GC贫乏型UDG的速度弥散则介于14 ± 3 km s⁻¹至21 ± 3 km s⁻¹之间。该差异对应R_e内的动力学质量相差2.5–4倍（GC丰度型：M_dyn(<R_e) ≈ 1.2–2.0 × 10⁹ M⊙；GC贫乏型：0.3–0.8 × 10⁹ M⊙）。值得注意的是，其中一例GC贫乏UDG（PUDG_R24）呈现出正在发生熄灭的光谱特征——[OII]等效宽度升高且Hα辐射受抑制，表明其正于约0.5 Gyr内向UDG相态演化，为人类提供了罕见的UDG实时形成的观测窗口。尤为关键的是，GC丰度与相空间位置（如星系团中心距离或下落速度）之间不存在相关性，从而排除了“GC丰度型UDG exclusively为在星系团并合前、已于高红移群环境内经历预处理并熄灭的卫星星系”这类模型。相反，σₑ与N_GC之间紧密的相关性强有力地支持暗晕质量起主导作用：GC丰度型UDG寄宿于质量更大的暗物质晕中（M_halo ∼ 10¹¹–10¹² M⊙），此类高质量晕不仅有利于早期高效形成球状星团，亦能维持更长的恒星形成历史，从而保留更高的恒星速度弥散。

这些发现标志着人们对星系熄灭过程及跨质量尺度结构演化的认知取得概念性突破。本研究首次在UDG中建立起GC系统丰度与动力学质量之间直接、基于观测的关联，且完全规避了光度学示踪体或异质数据集所引入的系统偏差，从而将GC计数从一种描述性现象升华为定量诊断晕势阱强度的有效工具。这一成果重新定义了UDG的本质：它并非仅由面亮度界定的同质星系类群，而是一个内部动力学深刻编码其组装历史的多样化群体——GC丰度型UDG实为大质量星系在低面亮度域的真实对应体，而GC贫乏型UDG则更接近于遭受潮汐剥离或环境致熄灭的矮星系。对理论模拟而言，本结果对流体动力学数值模拟提出了严格约束，要求其必须同时准确再现低密度环境下GC的形成效率与恒星运动学特征；对下一代巡天项目而言，本研究验证了GC丰度作为分辨低光度系统中晕质量的高效、强健示踪体的价值，为借助Rubin LSST与JWST等新一代设施，系统刻画宇宙时标上暗物质—重子物质关联奠定了坚实基础。
